**Origin & Habitat:**
Lupari come from vast tundras, dense coniferous forests, and snow-covered mountain ranges. They often live in remote villages or expansive hunting camps that exist in harmony with nature. Some clans travel nomadically through their territory, others build fixed but simple dwellings from wood, stone, and animal hides. The proximity to wildlife and the harsh environment shape their way of life and character.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Lupari live in close community – their social behavior is strongly shaped by pack structures. Loyalty, honor, and protection of the group stand above all else. Everyone in their "pack" has a role, whether hunter, keeper, speaker, or healer. Their culture values oral traditions, songs, and shared rituals. They meet strangers with reserved caution at first, but those who earn their trust are treated like a brother or sister. Lupari often venerate spirits of the hunt, the moon, or their ancestors.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Winter Coat** - The dense fur of the Lupari reliably protects them from cold. They are naturally resistant to icy environments as well as attacks involving frost or cold.
- **Pack Instinct** – Lupari instinctively sense the proximity of others of their folk, even without visual or auditory contact.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Kean Smell** – _4 AP_: A Lupari can once per day use their keen nose to track creatures within a radius of (Level x) 15 meters.
