**Origin & Habitat:**
Dwarves come from rocky mountain regions, deep cave kingdoms, and ancient underground cities hewn from stone across generations. They feel at home among stone, metal, and mountain air, but avoid large bodies of water and open plains. Their cities are architectural masterpieces with artful halls, glowing crystals, and ancient ancestor statues.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Dwarves are tradition-conscious, proud, and disciplined. Their culture is built on craftsmanship, ancestor veneration, and the principle of honor. Smithcraft, construction, and gemstone working enjoy high esteem. Every Dwarf knows their ancestral lineage and often carries intricately decorated crests or runes. Community and loyalty come first – betrayal is the gravest offense in their culture. Festivals are rarely celebrated, but when they are, with great devotion, strong mead, and loud singing.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Darkvision** – Decades in mines and underground halls have sharpened their eyes. Dwarves can still make out structures even in dim light or total darkness.
- **Hardy** – The body of a Dwarf is highly resilient. Many poisons affect them significantly less or not at all, and they receive one additional Health.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Stone Sense** – _4 AP_: By touching a stone or ore, Dwarves can sense its history and perceive hidden properties.
