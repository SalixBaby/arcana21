**Origin & Habitat:**
Humans come from the most diverse regions of the world – from vast plains, barren deserts, and frosty mountains to bustling coastal cities and fortified kingdoms. Their drive to explore, build, and conquer has led them to almost everywhere. They are often the founders of great cities, powerful empires, or flourishing trade routes, yet there are also human tribes that live in seclusion, close to nature, or nomadically.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Humans are adaptable, ambitious, and versatile. Their cultures range from strictly hierarchical to grassroots democratic, from highly educated to mystical-archaic. They are considered pragmatic and curious – often with the desire to leave something lasting behind, whether through art, knowledge, rulership, or technology. In the diversity of their societies there is room for almost every idea, belief system, and way of life.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Encourage** – Humans possess the almost supernatural gift of motivating themselves and their companions. Their words and example can ignite new courage even in hopeless moments.
- **Broad Spectrum** – Humans are versatile and adaptable. During character creation they may choose one additional Magical Domain, field of Knowledge, language, or equipment toolkit.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Glimmer of Hope** – _0 AP_: Humans can find a spark of hope even in the darkest moment. Once per combat, when the situation appears hopeless, they can receive a double bonus on a check that is thematically fitting.
