**Origin & Habitat:**
Vulpin prefer to live in temperate forests, at forest edges, or in hidden valleys, often near small villages or ruins. Their dwellings are cleverly camouflaged, frequently underground or built in harmonious connection with nature. They avoid open conflict and find themselves in border regions or as scouts between cultures. Some Vulpin roam alone, others in small, tightly bonded family clans.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Vulpin are considered cunning, charming, and silver-tongued. They place great value on self-determination, independence, and sophistication. Deception is not seen as dishonorable in their culture, but as a sign of intelligence – as long as no one is seriously harmed. Stories, masquerades, and hidden meanings shape their cultural life. In many communities clever action is valued above raw strength. Vulpin travel often and adapt effortlessly to new environments and cultures.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Light-Footed** – Vulpin move with near-silent elegance. Their steps rarely leave traces, making it exceedingly difficult for pursuers to pick up their trail.
- **Adaptable** – Whether in foreign cities, rugged wilderness, or unfamiliar societies – Vulpin instinctively blend into new circumstances and find a way to assert themselves faster than others.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Silver Tongue** – _4 AP_: Vulpin can once per day give their lies just the right touch to make them convincing. They receive a bonus on Deception.
