**Origin & Habitat:**
Elves come from ancient, magic-infused forests, hidden clearings, and half-forgotten ruins of the world. Their cities are often intricate, nature-bound structures that stretch through treetops or across moss-covered cliffs. Some live in crystalline caves or above the clouds in magically sustained settlements. Their society is old, rich in tradition, and full of respect for the world as it was – and as it could be.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Elves live in deep connection with nature, magic, and the flow of time. They are thoughtful, proud, and prone to a certain world-weariness. Their arts – whether music, poetry, swordsmanship, or spellcraft – are considered unparalleled. Elves strive for harmony, but are by no means weak: those who threaten their realm come to know their elegance in combat. They place great value on beauty, balance, and history – and they never forget.

**Body:**
- Category: Normal
- Size: 170–200 cm

**Age:**
- Lifespan: ~800 years
- Adult: 50 years

**Movement:**
- Running: 5 tiles
- Swimming: 2 tiles
- Jumping: 3 tiles
- Flying: -

**Folk Traits:**
- **Dream Watch** - Elves know no ordinary sleep. Instead they sink into deep meditation to renew body and mind and shed exhaustion.
- **Grace** - From birth, elves move with natural elegance. They are light, require little food, and often appear as though weightless.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Elven Eye** – _1 AP_: With their keen sight, elves can perceive things at great distances. They receive a bonus on Perception checks based on range or distance.
