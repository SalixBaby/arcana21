**Origin & Habitat:**
Fae originate from the interwoven fabric between the natural world and the so-called Otherworld – a reality of magic, emotion, and fleeting logic. They live in glittering groves, hidden mushroom villages, dancing misty meadows, or even on tiny floating islands. Many Fae settlements exist only during a full moon or appear to outsiders only under specific conditions.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Fae are whimsical, playful, and driven by deep curiosity. Their culture is based on exchange, promises, and stories – though they often understand truth and ownership differently from other folk. Music, dance, and magic permeate their daily lives. Some Fae are benevolent and helpful, others mischievous to the point of being dangerous. Their society has few hierarchies, yet older Fae beings enjoy natural respect, particularly those in harmony with nature or ancient magic.

**Body:**
- Category: Small

**Age:**

**Movement:**
- Running: 4 tiles
- Swimming: -
- Jumping: 1 tile

**Folk Traits:**
- **Binding Contracts** - The words of a Fae carry weight. Every pact, every promise binds them and their partners with invisible threads. Those who break a contract must reckon with severe consequences – whether through magic or fate itself.
- **Hidden Form** – Fae are masters of deception. With a thought they can veil their true features, making wings, radiance, and otherness disappear – and walk among humans or elves as though they had always belonged there.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Fly** – _1 AP_: Fae can fly as long as they are not carrying too much additional weight.
- **Shrink** – _1 AP_: Once per day, Fae can reduce their form to the size of a hand and vanish almost invisibly between cracks and shadows for up to one minute.

_Additional explanation for Flying:_
- *Flying requires concentration*
- *While flying, all attack or spell checks receive a triple penalty*
