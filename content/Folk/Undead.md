**Origin & Habitat:**
Undead are usually created through rare, dark magic that binds them to a specific purpose – whether guarding an ancient altar, protecting an age-old treasure, or seeking revenge for a suffered injustice. They have no homeland of their own, do not live in communities, and build no cities. Some Undead roam freely through the world, searching for a meaning or purpose they missed in life.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Undead possess no culture in the conventional sense. Their existence is shaped by their task or by memories of their former life. Many are forced to conceal their true nature to avoid being hunted or destroyed. Some learn to imitate human or other living behaviors in order to walk unrecognized among the living. Despite their undead existence they are capable of independent thought and decision-making – often driven by a melancholic or vengeful impulse.

**Age:**
- Variable – Undead do not age in the conventional sense; their physical condition is preserved as far as magic or decay permits.

- Running: Variable

**Folk Traits:**
- **Hidden Nature** – Undead must conceal their true form and nature to avoid being hunted. Deception, disguise, or imitating the living is often a matter of survival.
- **Magical Binding** – Many Undead are bound to a goal or location by the necromantic magic that created them. Some can gradually dissolve this binding as they learn to make use of their freedom.
- **Poison Immunity** - Undead can no longer be harmed by poison.
- ***Folk Trait** - Before your death you belonged to one of the other folk. Together with the GM it can be decided which folk traits you may carry over.*

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Devour** – _4 AP_: By consuming the flesh of a deceased creature, an Undead can receive fragments of its memories. On a successful check they gain a brief glimpse into the thoughts, feelings, or sensory impressions of the being before its death. The effect is usually fragmentary, confused, and colored by the perspective of the victim.
- ***Folk Ability** - Before your death you belonged to one of the other folk. Together with the GM it can be decided which folk ability you may carry over.*

_Additional explanation for Undead:_
- _Playing an Undead should be discussed with the GM beforehand._
- _Undead also have a body that can be damaged or destroyed._
- _Just because someone is Undead does not mean that person must be evil or wicked._
- _Undead possess no natural healing like the living; regeneration or repair often depends on magic._
- _Undead can be particularly vulnerable to certain magical effects or holy powers._
- _Interaction with the living requires caution – even friends could be startled or made suspicious by their existence._
- _Anything that is unclear, such as how healing works during a rest, can be discussed individually with the GM and should fit the hero's backstory._
