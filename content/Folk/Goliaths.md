**Origin & Habitat:**
Goliaths come from the most inhospitable heights of the world – snow-covered peaks, rugged mountain ranges, and wind-swept high plateaus. In these harsh regions they have learned to live in harmony with the elements, where food is scarce, air is thin, and survival is hard-won. They often live in small, proud communities that regard themselves as children of the mountains.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
The life of Goliaths is defined by strength, endurance, and competition. Their societies are small, tightly bonded, and shaped by the principle of merit – those who sacrifice for the community gain renown. Goliaths are freedom-loving, little hierarchical, and judge others by deeds rather than words. Stories of victories, daring ascents, or hardships survived are passed down from generation to generation. Despite their wildness, they possess a deep reverence for nature and the mountains, which they regard as sacred.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Stone Skin** – Goliaths naturally possess an exceptionally resilient skin and bone structure that protects them from 1 physical damage once per day.
- **Competitive Instinct** – They always seek the challenge. Goliaths are difficult to discourage and thrive particularly when pitting themselves against worthy opponents.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Mountain Strength** – _1 AP_: Once per day, Goliaths can put all their strength into one attack or effort. They receive a double bonus on a check requiring raw strength (e.g. lifting, pushing, shattering).
