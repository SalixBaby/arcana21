Every hero comes from a folk – an ancient lineage shaped by culture, environment, and magic. Whether from the shadow of deep forests, the halls of mountains, the alleys of cities, or the still waters of swamps: a folk shapes not only the body of a being, but also its senses, its language, its thinking.

A folk grants you unique abilities, instinctive strengths, and a history that reaches deeper than your own name. Some folk stand for pride and honor, others for cunning, survival, or transformation.

Choose wisely – for where you come from shapes how you fight, think, and feel.

- [[./Aldori (Deer Folk)|Aldori (Deer Folk)]] – Dignified forest beings known for calm, vigilance, and deep connection to nature.
- [[./Anuri (Frog Folk)|Anuri (Frog Folk)]] – Adaptable swamp dwellers with powerful leaps, keen perception, and a close bond to water, mist, and damp wilderness.
- [[./Corvum (Raven Folk)|Corvum (Raven Folk)]] – Intelligent and observant collectors of secrets, known for their foresight, art of memory, and dark symbolism.
- [[./Drakes (Dragon Folk)|Drakes (Dragon Folk)]] – Proud and powerful descendants of draconic lineages, shaped by inner fire, strength of will, and venerable heritage.
- [[./Dwarves|Dwarves]] – Steadfast masters of craftsmanship, deeply rooted in tradition, stone, and unyielding will.
- [[./Elves|Elves]] – Long-lived guardians of nature and magic, sensitive, graceful, and deeply connected to the world and its cycles.
- [[./Fae|Fae]] – Beings beyond clear rules, playful, unpredictable, and deeply intertwined with emotion, illusion, and otherworldly magic.
- [[./Gnomes|Gnomes]] – Curious tinkerers and thinkers who place knowledge, mechanics, and ingenuity above raw strength.
- [[./Goliaths|Goliaths]] – Towering survivors from harsh regions, known for physical strength, endurance, and a sense of honor.
- [[./Halflings|Halflings]] – Small but tough adventurers with great luck, social skill, and surprising resilience.
- [[./Humans|Humans]] – Adaptable all-rounders with great ambition, diversity, and the ability to find their footing anywhere.
- [[./Lumari (Dove Folk)|Lumari (Dove Folk)]] – Peace-loving, community-oriented flyers known for endurance, navigation, and diplomatic skill.
- [[./Lupari (Wolf Folk)|Lupari (Wolf Folk)]] – Pack-bound hunters with sharp senses, loyalty, and an instinctive understanding of the hunt and movement.
- [[./Parvus (Mouse Folk)|Parvus (Mouse Folk)]] – Small survivors, nimble, cautious, and surprisingly brave when it counts.
- [[./Strixan (Owl Folk)|Strixan (Owl Folk)]] – Night-sighted observers with sharp minds, patience, and an inclination toward knowledge and foresight.
- [[./Tabaxi (Cat Folk)|Tabaxi (Cat Folk)]] – Curious, nimble adventurers with a strong drive for freedom, skill, and playful arrogance.
- [[./Tieflings|Tieflings]] – Marked by foreign powers, they carry a heritage of distrust, inner conflict, and hidden strength.
- [[./Undead|Undead]] – Beings beyond life and death, sustained by foreign forces, with a distorted perception of time and existence.
- [[./Ursa (Bear Folk)|Ursa (Bear Folk)]] – Massive, steadfast figures known for raw strength, protective instinct, and endurance.
- [[./Veldri (Rabbit Folk)|Veldri (Rabbit Folk)]] – Fast, nervous, but alert beings who rely on reflexes, flight, and instinct.
- [[./Vulpin (Fox Folk)|Vulpin (Fox Folk)]] – Cunning survivors known for deception, adaptation, and sharp wit.
