**Origin & Habitat:**
Drakes descend from ancient dragon lineages whose blood mingled with that of mortals. Their homeland is rugged mountain ranges, barren steppes, or desolate volcanic lands where the heat of the earth can still be felt. Some clans have built their settlements in fortresses of black basalt or shimmering obsidian, while others travel the world in nomadic tribes. Their people are proud, honor-conscious, and deeply connected to the elements – they see themselves as heirs to the ancient power of dragons.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Drakes live in a society shaped by tradition and clan structures. Honor, strength, and loyalty are the highest values. They are proud warriors, but also sages who preserve ancient knowledge of dragon hoards and the secrets of the elements. Many Drakes maintain rituals of fire or air to honor their ancestors. Strangers often perceive them as intimidating, yet behind their rough exterior lies a deep sense of duty. Those who earn the trust of a Drake gain a companion for life – and a merciless enemy should that trust ever be broken.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Dragon Heritage** – Ancient dragon blood flows through the veins of the Drakes, granting them a natural resistance to the element of their bloodline.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Dragon Breath** – _2 AP_: A Drakes can once per day unleash the raw power of their heritage and release a cone-shaped breath attack of their element (fire, acid, lightning, depending on bloodline).
