**Origin & Habitat:**
Aldori come from ancient, deep forests, enchanted clearings, and the wild borderlands between nature and magic. They live in open groves, overgrown tree dwellings, or simple wooden settlements, often hidden beneath dense foliage or protected by natural illusions. Their homeland is usually rich in ancient myths, talking animals, and nature spirits.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Aldori are considered peaceful observers and wise wanderers. They live in harmony with their surroundings, pay heed to balance, and avoid unnecessary violence. Their society is matriarchal in nature, with a deep respect for elders and dream interpreters. Art, music, and the telling of visions and dreams hold great importance. Many Aldori are gifted healers, seers, or forest guides. They avoid industrial civilization but are drawn to pure magic.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Antlers** – Aldori naturally bear antlers that grow larger and stronger with age. They serve as a status symbol in their culture, but can also be used as a weapon in combat.
- **Forest Bond** – Aldori possess an innate connection to nature and instinctively sense magical or poisoned plants.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Forest Glide** – _1 AP_: Aldori use the strength of their legs to move with ease through forests and uneven terrain. Their Movement is not reduced by natural terrain this round.
