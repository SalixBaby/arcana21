**Origin & Habitat:**
Tieflings descend from ancient bloodlines once connected to demonic or infernal powers – whether through pacts, curses, or ancient magic. Today they live scattered across cities, border regions, or hidden communities, where they either attempt to conceal their heritage or wear it with pride. Their dwellings are frequently places with a certain mystique or troubled past, often in borderlands between the natural and the supernatural.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Tieflings frequently grow up in a society that meets them with distrust or fear. As a result many of them develop a strong sense of self, a sharp mind, and a feel for deception or manipulation. Some withdraw from society, others meet prejudice with pride, charm, or provocation. Tieflings tend to forge their own paths, rarely follow rules without reason, and are often found in the realms of magic, art, or the underworld. Many Tieflings carry inner struggles between light and darkness – making them at once vulnerable and fascinating.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Infernal Heritage** – A spark of infernal power pulses through their blood. This heritage makes them more resistant to flames and heat.
- **Alluring Aura** – Tieflings possess a presence that is equally fascinating and unsettling. They draw attention to themselves whether they want to or not.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Hellfire** – _1 AP_: When a Tiefling takes damage, they can once per day unleash the infernal power smoldering within them. As a reaction, a spark of hellish energy strikes back and hits the attacker.

_Additional explanation for Hellfire:_
- *Irrevocably hits an opponent who has dealt damage to them*
- *Deals (Level x) infernal damage*
