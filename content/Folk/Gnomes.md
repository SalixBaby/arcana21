**Origin & Habitat:**
Gnomes come from ancient forests, mist-shrouded moors, and hidden hills, often near places of natural magic or ancient knowledge. Some live in mushroom-like treehouses, others in winding cave networks beneath the earth. They prefer secluded regions, which they protect from curious eyes with small illusions and clever mechanisms.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Gnomes are known for their enthusiasm for tinkering, stories, and magic. They are considered eccentric but warm-hearted, and place great value on family, craftsmanship, and knowledge. Their humor is playful, often full of wordplay and double meanings. Many gnomes document their thoughts in journals, build small contraptions to pass the time, or tell stories that can last for hours. Their society is egalitarian, but they particularly admire wise or creative minds.

**Body:**
- Category: Small
- Size: 70–100 cm

**Age:**
- Lifespan: 250 years

**Movement:**
- Running: 4 tiles

**Folk Traits:**
- **Darkvision** - Gnomes have a keen sense for shadows and contours. Even in near-total darkness they can still find their way around remarkably well.
- **Tenacious** - Their unyielding spirit makes them resistant to influence. Mental spells have a penalty on their effect against them.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Sharp Mind** – _4 AP_: Once per day, gnomes can pause, study the situation carefully, and draw new conclusions. On a successful check that is thematically fitting, they perceive additional details, connections, or hidden aspects that would remain concealed from others.
