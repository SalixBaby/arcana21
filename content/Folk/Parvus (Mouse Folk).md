**Origin & Habitat:**
Parvus live mostly hidden in densely overgrown forests, underground caves, or abandoned ruins. They build winding tunnel networks, intricate nests, or small villages from natural materials – often well concealed from larger folk. They feel particularly at ease in communities that offer protection and structure, whether deep in the forest, beneath the earth, or even in the shadow of great cities.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Parvus are extremely social beings who rely on cooperation, mutual aid, and the passing on of knowledge. They value order, stories, and rituals, many of which are passed down orally. Despite their small size they are brave and exceptionally clever – many Parvus are nimble messengers, skilled thieves, wise healers, or detail-loving archivists. In their culture loyalty and community spirit are regarded as the highest virtues, but caution toward strangers has also become deeply ingrained in their behavior.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Tremorsense** – Parvus perceive the finest vibrations in the ground, allowing them to orient themselves reliably even in total darkness or without sight.
- **Gnawing Teeth** – The powerful teeth of Parvus are far more than mere tools. They can effortlessly bite through ropes, restraints, or even thin metal.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Adrenaline** – _0 AP_: Parvus can once per day mobilize hidden reserves and draw forward 2 additional Action Points from their next turn.
