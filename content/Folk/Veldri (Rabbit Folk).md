**Origin & Habitat:**
Veldri live in vast grasslands, hilly forests, open plains, or at the edges of dense wilderness. They prefer regions with rich vegetation and numerous hiding spots, often in earthen burrows or small, tightly bonded settlements that blend harmoniously into the landscape. Their dwellings are mostly underground or fitted with natural camouflage to protect them from enemies.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Veldri are known for their liveliness, caution, and social strength. They live in close communities built on trust, mutual aid, and quick thinking and acting. Stories, dances, and the sharing of food hold great importance in their culture. Despite their physical fragility they are brave and exceptionally nimble. Many Veldri have a natural urge to discover new things – whether through travel, stories, or unusual friendships.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Inner Conflict** – Veldri live in a constant tension between caution and curiosity. They weigh dangers carefully, yet their insatiable curiosity often drives them to take risks regardless.
- **Nervous Disposition** – Veldri react with lightning speed to surprises or new situations, granting them a bonus on Initiative checks.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Survival Instinct** – _0 AP_: Veldri can once per day receive a double bonus on a check to escape, hide, or survive.
