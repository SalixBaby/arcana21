**Origin & Habitat:**
Lumari come from vast high plateaus, wind-swept slopes, and airy terrace cities they have built at lofty heights. Many of their ancient cities are found on free-floating rocks, in abandoned sky monasteries, or on expansive tree platforms. Their closeness to the sky shapes their thinking – they are considered spiritual, peace-loving, and filled with a natural dignity.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Lumari maintain a calm, meditative way of life and value music, harmony, and balance. Many of them appear as wandering pilgrims, messengers, or sky priests. Communication is essential to the Lumari – they value clear, peaceful words and are considered excellent diplomats and mediators. Their song is regarded as sacred, and some of their melodies are passed down across generations.

**Body:**
- Category: Normal
- Size: 150–170 cm

**Age:**
- Lifespan: 90 years
- Adult: 16 years

**Movement:**
- Running: 3 tiles
- Swimming: -
- Jumping: 1 tile
- Flying: 6 tiles

**Folk Traits:**
- **Calming Presence** - The aura of the Lumari has a soothing effect and can bring even agitated minds to rest.
- **Homing Instinct** - Lumari always find their way back home. They know at any point in time which direction their home lies. What or who their home is should be clarified with the GM beforehand.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Fly** – _1 AP_: Lumari can fly as long as they are not carrying too much additional weight.

_Additional explanation for Flying:_
- *Flying requires concentration*
- *While flying, all attack or spell checks receive a triple penalty*
