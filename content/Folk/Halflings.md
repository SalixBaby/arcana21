**Origin & Habitat:**
Halflings come from fertile valleys, gentle hills, and densely overgrown river landscapes. Their villages are often hidden away from the great trade roads, mostly in earth houses or small groves surrounded by gardens, fruit trees, and babbling streams. They prefer quiet, peaceful areas where they can lead a simple but enjoyable life.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Halflings are a cheerful, sociable folk who value community, good food, and stories. Festivals and family gatherings hold great importance for them, as does the sharing of harvest, time, and wit. Despite their easygoing nature they are considered surprisingly tough, nimble, and clever. Many halflings have a natural curiosity about the wide world out there – some are drawn to travel, others make a name for themselves as scouts, messengers, or even adventurers. They avoid conflict, but know how to hold their own when necessary.

**Body:**
- Category: Small
- Size: 80–110 cm

**Age:**
- Lifespan: 120 years

**Movement:**
- Running: 4 tiles

**Folk Traits:**
- **Unshakeable** - Halflings cannot be afflicted with fear, whether through magic or ordinary intimidation.
- **Nimble** - Thanks to their small stature, halflings slip through even tight gaps and can dart effortlessly between the legs of larger opponents.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Lucky** – _1 AP_: Once per day, halflings may press their luck and reroll a failed roll.
