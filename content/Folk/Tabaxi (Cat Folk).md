**Origin & Habitat:**
Tabaxi come from dense jungles, mist-shrouded rainforests, and sun-drenched savannas. Their settlements are often hidden in treetops or on rocky elevated terrain, from where they can survey their surroundings with sharp eyes. They live in seclusion and avoid permanent ties to a territory – many Tabaxi are nomads, hunters, or gatherers who travel in small family groups.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Tabaxi are curious, proud, and agile beings with a deep sense of freedom and individuality. They value stories, secrets, and unusual discoveries above all else. Many of them keep travel journals, collect strange objects, or chase memories. Their culture is shaped by movement, adaptation, and beauty – whether in dance, the hunt, or ritual combat. Fame and stories are valued as a legacy far above material possessions.

**Folk Traits:**
- **Curiosity Drive** – Tabaxi feel an insatiable urge to discover new things. Their curiosity grants them valuable experiences, but brings them just as often into precarious situations.
- **Land on Their Paws** – Tabaxi often land cat-like on their feet after a fall, halving fall damage and preventing them from being knocked to the ground.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Balancing Act** – _1 AP_: Tabaxi can automatically resist stumbling, falling, or any effect that would knock them to the ground.
