**Origin & Habitat:**
Anuri come from misty swamps, moss-covered rainforests, and flooded lowlands. Their villages often consist of wood and plant constructions built on stilts or fastened to trees. Water is not merely their habitat but part of their culture – many Anuri customs and festivals revolve around rain, rivers, or other forms of water.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Anuri live mostly in tightly bonded communities where decisions are made collectively. They are known for their patience, calm way of life, and keen sense of observation. Their art often consists of sung stories and rhythmic drumming that imitates the croaking of frogs. Many Anuri venerate nature spirits, particularly those connected to water and fertility.

**Body:**
- Category: Small

**Age:**
- Lifespan: 50 years

- Running: 4 tiles

**Folk Traits:**
- **Amphibious** - Anuri feel as much at home in the water as on land. They can dive indefinitely and maintain their bearings effortlessly even in murky depths.
- **Mucous Skin** - The moist, smooth skin of the Anuri makes it difficult to grab or restrain them with bare hands.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Leap** – _4 AP_: Anuri can use their powerful legs to jump omnidirectionally and twice as far as normal.
- **Sticky Tongue** – _1 AP_: Their long, flexible tongue allows Anuri to pull objects or small creatures from up to 3 tiles away toward them.
