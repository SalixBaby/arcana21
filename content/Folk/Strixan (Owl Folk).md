**Origin & Habitat:**
Strixan come from dense, silent forests, cool high mountains, and secluded ruins of ancient temple complexes. They prefer places with a wide field of view and little disturbance, often at great heights or deep within magically infused nature. Their villages are found in treetops or on rocky cliffs and are usually well camouflaged. Strixan often live in seclusion and appear to strangers unexpectedly – as silent as the flight of their wings.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Strixan are considered vigilant, wise, and tradition-conscious. They place great value on observation, inner balance, and the study of stars, dreams, and ancient prophecies. Many Strixan see themselves as guardians of ancient knowledge or as mediators between nature and magic. Conversations among them are often quiet and considered – every word carries weight. Education, meditation, and spiritual journeys are a central part of their culture. Their music often sounds otherworldly and airy, almost whispering.

**Body:**
- Category: Normal
- Size: 150–170 cm

**Age:**
- Lifespan: 120 years
- Adult: 15 years

**Movement:**
- Running: 3 tiles
- Swimming: -
- Jumping: 1 tile
- Flying: 6 tiles

**Folk Traits:**
- **Darkvision** - Strixan possess sharp, light-sensitive eyes that grant them clear vision even in faint moonlight. In total darkness they perceive movement and outlines better than most other folk.
- **Silent Flight** – Their feather-covered wings move almost soundlessly. This makes them inconspicuous in movement and harder to detect as long as they do not step directly into the light.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Fly** – _1 AP_: Strixan can fly as long as they are not carrying too much additional weight.

_Additional explanation for Flying:_
- *Flying requires concentration*
- *While flying, all attack or spell checks receive a triple penalty*
