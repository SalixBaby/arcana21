**Origin & Habitat:**
Ursa come from rugged, forested mountain ranges, cold coniferous forests, and icy tundras. They build their settlements in massive wooden or stone structures, deeply rooted in nature and frequently near sacred places such as ancient stone circles or ancestor groves. Many Ursa live in small tribal communities that are self-sufficient and observe the rhythms of the seasons. They avoid overcrowded cities, though some live as wanderers, traders, or protectors along remote routes.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Ursa are calm, powerful beings with a strong sense of family and deep connection to nature. They believe in the wisdom of the elders, in stories passed down across generations, and in rituals honoring spirits, ancestors, and the wilderness. They tend to avoid conflict – but when they fight, they do so with resolve and unwavering strength. Hospitality, protection of the weak, and a strong sense of honor are core values. Music, craftsmanship, and traditional tattooing play an important role in their culture.
