**Origin & Habitat:**
Corvum prefer to live in elevated regions: on steep cliffs, in rugged mountains, or the highest towers of ancient cities. Some of them roam the world as nomads, always in search of knowledge, stories, or lost relics. Their settlements are often built vertically, with suspension bridges, nests, and lookout points – places suited to flight.

**Culture & Way of Life:**
Corvum are considered intelligent, curious, and a little eccentric. Their society places great value on the gathering and preservation of knowledge. Many of them are storytellers, oracles, spies, or librarians. Their humor is sharp-tongued, their loyalty deep – but they forget nothing. It is said that a Corvum carries every word they have ever heard within their mind.
They have a particular fascination for shiny objects, passed-down songs, and secret truths.

**Body:**
- Category: Normal
- Size: 150–170 cm

**Age:**
- Lifespan: 80 years
- Adult: 15 years

**Movement:**
- Running: 3 tiles
- Swimming: -
- Jumping: 1 tile
- Flying: 6 tiles

**Folk Traits:**
- **Darkvision** - Corvum are accustomed to living in dim environments. Even in near-total darkness they can still orient themselves with remarkable ease.
- **Sharp Memory** - Faces in particular are burned deeply into their memory. A Corvum rarely forgets who they have encountered – be it friend or foe.

**Folk Abilities:**
- **Fly** – _1 AP_: Corvum can fly as long as they are not carrying too much additional weight.

_Additional explanation for Flying:_
- *Flying requires concentration*
- *While flying, all attack or spell checks receive a triple penalty*
