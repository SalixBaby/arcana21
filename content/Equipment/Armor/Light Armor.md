> _The **Light Armor** offers a good compromise between protection and mobility. It consists of flexible materials such as hardened leather or light metal mesh, allowing a hero to remain agile while still having some defense against physical damage. This armor is particularly suited for adventurers who need to move quickly or remain undetected, such as thieves, rangers, or light warriors._

- **Protection**: Reduces physical damage by 1, up to 2 times before requiring repair.
- **Typical Materials**: Hardened leather, leather segments, light chainmail.
- **Donning / Doffing**: 1 minute / 30 seconds.
- **Repair**: Can be repaired with a [[../Toolkits/Leathercraft Toolkit|Leathercraft Toolkit]] or by a professional leatherworker. Takes 1 hour.

- **Disadvantage**: Reduces [[../../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]] by 1.
- **Requirement**: None.
