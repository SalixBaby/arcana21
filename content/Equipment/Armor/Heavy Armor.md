> _The **Heavy Armor** offers maximum protection against physical damage, but is the heaviest and most cumbersome armor one can wear. It consists of massive metal plates and ensures that the wearer is nearly invulnerable to most physical attacks. However, this protection comes at a price – freedom of movement is severely restricted and speed is considerably reduced. This armor is ideal for those who wish to throw themselves into the heart of battle and serve as an unshakeable protective wall._

- **Protection**: Reduces physical damage by 1.
- **Typical Materials**: Plate armor, full suit of hardened steel.
- **Donning / Doffing**: 10 minutes / 5 minutes. (Can be halved with assistance)
- **Maintenance**: Must be maintained or repaired with a [[../Toolkits/Smithing Toolkit|Smithing Toolkit]] or by a professional blacksmith at least every 5 days. Takes 2 hours.

- **Disadvantage**: Reduces [[../../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]] by 3 and flying is not possible.
- **Requirement**: Minimum Strength of 3.
