> _The **Medium Armor** offers balanced protection without severely restricting freedom of movement. It combines reinforced leather and metal plates to protect the wearer against harder strikes, while still ensuring a degree of mobility. This type of armor is often favored by experienced warriors, mercenaries, and paladins who value a mix of protection and maneuverability._

- **Protection**: Reduces physical damage by 1, up to 4 times before requiring repair.
- **Typical Materials**: Chainmail, scale armor, hardened leather plates with metal reinforcement.
- **Donning / Doffing**: 5 minutes / 1 minute. (Can be halved with assistance)
- **Repair**: Can be repaired with a [[../Toolkits/Smithing Toolkit|Smithing Toolkit]] or by a professional blacksmith. Takes 8 hours.

- **Disadvantage**: Reduces [[../../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]] by 2.
- **Requirement**: Minimum Strength of 1.
