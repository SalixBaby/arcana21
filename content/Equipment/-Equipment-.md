> _**Equipment** is far more than merely practical tools – it is an essential part of the hero's journey. Whether weapons, armor, or specialized toolkits, the right equipment can make the difference between success and failure. Equipment is tailored to the challenges of the world and offers heroes the opportunity to prepare optimally for every situation._

[[./Weapons/-Weapons-|Weapons]] offer a wide range of tactical possibilities, depending on their properties and abilities. They allow heroes to overcome their enemies both in melee and from a distance, or to move silently and inconspicuously through dangerous territory.

[[./Armor/-Armor-|Armor]] protects the wearer from physical damage, but every type of armor also brings restrictions, particularly regarding speed and mobility. The choice of armor influences how well a hero can deal and take damage in combat, and requires strategic consideration.

[[./Toolkits/-Toolkits-|Toolkits]] expand a hero's abilities outside of direct combat. These toolkits – from the Thieves' Toolkit to the Herbalism Toolkit to the Navigator's Toolkit – provide tools and opportunities to complete specific tasks, solve puzzles, and overcome special challenges.

Together, weapons, armor, and toolkits form a broad range of **equipment** that enables the heroes of **Arcana21** to survive in a dangerous world and prevail in their adventures.
