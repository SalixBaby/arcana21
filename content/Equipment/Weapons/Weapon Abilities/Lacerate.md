> *This ability allows deep wounds to be inflicted with a weapon, weakening the opponent over time. It is particularly effective against unprotected or lightly armored enemies.*

- Cost: 1 AP
- Effect: 1 Bleed (1 damage at the start of the turn; stackable; one Bleed is lost per round)
- Duration: 1 round
- Requirement: The original attack must hit and deal damage
