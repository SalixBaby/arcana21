> *With this ability, the hero can temporarily incapacitate an opponent by striking them directly or hitting them with a precise technique. Stunned opponents lose a turn or are more vulnerable to attacks.*

- Cost: 1 AP
- Effect: 1 Stun (Stun reduces AP for one turn; stackable; one Stun is lost per round)
- Duration: 1 round
- Requirement: The original attack must hit
