>*Alongside their properties, weapons in **Arcana21** have special **Weapon Abilities** that offer the hero additional options in combat. These abilities are targeted techniques that maximize the use of the weapon, whether through lacerating opponents, stunning enemies, or precisely piercing armor. Some abilities allow attacks to be better deflected, while others favor precise hits.*

Weapon abilities grant heroes greater tactical depth by giving them the option to perform special maneuvers and specifically target the strengths and weaknesses of their opponents. They not only provide more control over the course of combat, but also reward strategic thinking and skilled weapon use.

*(Weapon abilities can only be used by combat classes and must be declared before the respective attack.)*
