> _With brutal force or uncontrolled energy, you strike not only your intended target but also cause damage to everything immediately beside it. This ability is dangerous – for your opponents, but sometimes also for the surroundings._

- **Cost:** 0 AP
- **Effect:** Deals 1 additional damage to the target or to a target directly adjacent to the original target.
- **Duration:** 1 attack
- **Requirement:** The original attack must hit.
