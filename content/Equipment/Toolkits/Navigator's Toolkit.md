> _The **Navigator's Toolkit** is essential for every seafarer or adventurer who must cover great distances without losing their bearings._

The toolkit contains:

- **Sea Charts**: A collection of common and rare maps of the surrounding area.
- **Astrolabe**: A small device for determining position using the stars.
- **Compass**: An accurate compass for always knowing one's direction.
- **Logbook**: A notebook for recording the journey and course corrections.
- **Hourglass**: For measuring time at sea or while traveling.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Navigation checks
