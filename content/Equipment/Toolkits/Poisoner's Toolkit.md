> _The **Poisoner's Toolkit** contains everything an alchemist or rogue needs to produce lethal and non-lethal poisons. It is suited for both subtle assassinations and the preparation of defenses._

The toolkit contains:

- **Glass Pipettes and Vials**: For precise dosing of toxic substances.
- **Toxic Powder and Herbs**: Base materials for simple poisons.
- **Micro Mortar and Pestle**: For processing fine powders and ingredients.
- **Safety Mask**: A small leather mask to protect oneself from fumes and splashes.
- **Antidote Recipe**: A list of the most common antidotes in case something goes wrong.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Poison checks
