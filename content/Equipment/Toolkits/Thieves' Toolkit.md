> _The **Thieves' Toolkit** is the indispensable equipment for any skilled pickpocket or shadowrunner. It contains everything needed to pick locks, disarm traps, or slip unnoticed into secured rooms._

The toolkit contains:

- **Lockpicks**: A selection of fine tools for opening locks of various kinds.
- **Cutting Wire**: A thin, strong wire, perfect for severing ropes or small mechanical parts in traps.
- **Charcoal Powder**: For detecting hidden pressure plates or levers.
- **Velvet Pouch**: For silently collecting small valuables.
- **Hook and Grappler**: A small, foldable hook for opening doors or reaching objects.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Lockpicking checks
