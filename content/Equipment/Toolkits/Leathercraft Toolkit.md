> _The **Leathercraft Toolkit** is ideal for all those who need to be able to repair their light armor or other leather equipment themselves._

The toolkit contains:

- **Cutting Knife**: A precise knife for cutting and trimming leather.
- **Hole Punch**: For creating perfectly sized holes for rivets and buckles.
- **Needle and Thread**: Sturdy materials for securely and durably stitching leather.
- **Polishing Tool**: For smoothing and finishing the worked surface.
- **Leather Oil**: For protecting and maintaining equipment, keeping the leather supple.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Repair of leather items without a check
