> _The **Musician's Toolkit** is aimed at bards, minstrels, and other sound artists. It contains the most important utensils for producing music – whether for entertainment, ceremony, or as a magical medium._

The toolkit contains:

- **Miniature Instrument (of choice)**: A small lute, flute, or drum.
- **Tuning Rod**: A simple resonance rod for pitch control.
- **Sheet Roll**: Blank parchment for recording personal compositions.
- **Maintenance Kit**: Oil and cloth for caring for the instrument.
- **Amulet of Harmony**: A small, often symbolic piece of jewelry for inspiration and recognizing fellow music enthusiasts.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Music checks
