> _The **Tinkerer's Toolkit** is perfect for inventors, hobbyists, and craftsmen who develop complex devices and machines. It enables the repair and invention of new contraptions._

The toolkit contains:

- **Fine Tools**: Screwdrivers, pliers, and small hammers for delicate work.
- **Metal and Wire Supply**: Small metal parts and wires for repair or construction.
- **Springs and Gears**: Various components for mechanical devices.
- **Magnifying Goggles**: Special goggles with magnifying lenses for intricate work.
- **Lubricating Oil**: For greasing moving parts.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Mechanics checks
