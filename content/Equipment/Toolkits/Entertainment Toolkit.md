> _Whether at a campfire, on a long journey, or in a tavern – this toolkit contains everything needed to pass the time, lift the mood, or compete in small contests. Ideal for heroes who know that laughter and play are just as important as sword and spell._

**The toolkit contains:**

- **Playing Card Deck**: A complete card game featuring symbols from all kingdoms. Perfect for games of chance, bluffing, and memory.
- **Pair of Dice**: Two well-weighted dice carved from bone – ideal for gambling, fortune-telling, or settling disputes.
- **Puzzle Game**: A small wooden puzzle for brain-teasing and stress relief.
- **Marble Pouch**: A handful of colored glass marbles for simple parlor games or improvised tricks.
- **Mood Song Flyer**: A folded sheet with three well-known drinking songs for singing along or performing.
- **Distraction Die**: Easy to conceal in one hand – perfect for sleight of hand tricks or distracting opponents.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Luck checks
