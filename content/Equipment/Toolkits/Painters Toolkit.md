> _The **Painters Toolkit** is for artists and creatives who wish to capture their experiences and thoughts in colors and images. It is excellent for travelers who want to record landscapes or special moments._

The toolkit contains:

- **Brush Set**: A set of fine and coarse brushes for different stroke widths.
- **Paint Pots**: A selection of primary colors that can easily be mixed.
- **Canvas Roll**: A small, portable canvas for use on the go.
- **Mixing Palette**: For blending colors and shades.
- **Charcoal and Chalk**: For sketches and preparatory drawings.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Painting checks
