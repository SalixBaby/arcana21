> _The **Excavation Toolkit** is aimed at historians, treasure hunters, and archaeologists who wish to recover remnants of past eras. It enables careful uncovering, documenting, and securing of delicate finds._

The toolkit contains:

- **Mini Spade and Chisel**: Precision tools for delicate excavations.
- **Brush and Bellows**: For uncovering and cleaning intricate structures.
- **Notebook with Scales**: For quick sketches and find documentation.
- **Sample Tubes**: For storing smaller finds or soil samples.
- **Folding Magnifier**: For initial examinations directly on site.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Investigation checks
