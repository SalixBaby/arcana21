>*The **Disguise Toolkit** is indispensable for spies, actors, or adventurers who wish to remain unrecognized. It contains everything needed to quickly and convincingly alter one's appearance.*

The toolkit contains:
- **Wigs and False Beards**: Various hairpieces for changing one's appearance.
- **Makeup Kit**: Colors and powders for altering the face or simulating wounds.
- **Costume Pieces**: Easily assembled garments representing various professions or roles.
- **Forged Documents**: Blank identification papers that can be quickly falsified.
- **Contact Lenses**: Colored lenses for changing eye color.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:
- Bonus on all Disguise checks
