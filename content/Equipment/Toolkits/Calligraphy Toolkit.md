> _The **Calligraphy Toolkit** is for scribes and artists who wish to create works of art from words and symbols. It is suited for writing letters, drawing magical glyphs, or producing official documents._

The toolkit contains:

- **Calligraphy Quill**: An artfully crafted quill for fine lines and details.
- **Ink Bottles**: Several bottles of black and colored ink.
- **Parchment Scrolls**: High-quality parchment for letters, spell formulas, or drawings.
- **Ruler and Protractor**: For drawing perfect lines and angles.
- **Sealing Wax**: For sealing important documents with a personal seal.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Scribing checks
