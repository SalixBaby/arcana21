>*The **Cartography Toolkit** is for those adventurers and explorers who wish to chart unknown territories and create precise maps. It is indispensable for navigating foreign lands or recording mysterious locations.*

The toolkit contains:
- **Map Paper**: Sturdy, easily transportable paper for detailed maps.
- **Divider and Ruler**: Precise tools for mapping distances.
- **Scalpel**: For finely cutting and adjusting maps.
- **Compass**: A small, reliable compass for orientation.
- **Pencil and Drawing Charcoal**: For sketching drafts and creating final designs.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:
- Bonus on all Navigation checks
