> _The **Smithing Toolkit** is suited for the maintenance and repair of medium and heavy armor as well as weapons. With this toolkit, cracks and dents can be mended and parts reinforced. It is an indispensable tool for any warrior who wants to ensure their equipment remains reliable even in the harshest battles._

The toolkit contains:

- **Smithing Hammer**: A sturdy hammer used for mending dents and edges.
- **Tongs**: For a secure grip when working on hot or difficult metal parts.
- **Whetstone**: For sharpening and maintaining weapons and other cutting tools.
- **File**: For smoothing edges and performing minor repairs on metal armor.
- **Polishing Agent**: For shining and protecting metal surfaces against corrosion.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Repair of metal items without a check
