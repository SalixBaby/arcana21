> _The **Research Toolkit** supports scholars and explorers in analyzing unknown substances, conducting simple experiments, or gathering new insights. It is suited for laboratory work on the go._

The toolkit contains:

- **Test Tubes with Rack**: For chemical or magical reactions.
- **Parchment and Charcoal**: For recording observations.
- **Analysis Powder**: A versatile substance for testing liquids.
- **Measuring Spoon and Tweezers**: For precise work with samples.
- **Symbol Chart**: A reference tool for alchemical or arcane patterns.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Research checks
