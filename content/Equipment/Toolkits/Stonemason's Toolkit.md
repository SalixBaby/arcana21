> _The **Stonemason's Toolkit** is for craftsmen and master builders who create magnificent works from stone. It is suited for both rough construction work and intricate decorations._

The toolkit contains:

- **Chisel Set**: Various chisels for coarse and fine work.
- **Sledgehammer**: A heavy hammer for working stone.
- **Straightedge and Protractor**: For precise cuts and perfect angles.
- **Grinding Stones**: For smoothing and polishing surfaces.
- **Charcoal Drawing Set**: For sketching designs onto stone.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Stonemasonry checks
