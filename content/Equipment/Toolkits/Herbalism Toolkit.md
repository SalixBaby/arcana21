> _The **Herbalism Toolkit** is for healers and naturalists who are searching for medicinal plants and herbs. It enables the gathering and processing of plants into healing salves and potions._

The toolkit contains:

- **Plant Cutter**: A small knife for cutting herbs and roots.
- **Drying Pouches**: Several breathable pouches for drying plants.
- **Plant Press**: For preserving leaves and blossoms.
- **Herb Guide**: A handy book with illustrations and descriptions of common medicinal herbs.
- **Plant Jar**: A small jar for storing freshly gathered plants.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Nature checks
