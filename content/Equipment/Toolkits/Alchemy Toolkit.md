> _The **Alchemy Toolkit** is indispensable for anyone who masters the art of alchemy and wishes to craft potions, elixirs, and poisons on their travels. This toolkit provides all the tools needed to create mystical substances and brew healing as well as combat tinctures._

The toolkit contains:

- **Distillation Flask**: A small glass container with a sturdy holder for extracting essences and heating liquids.
- **Mortar and Pestle**: A resilient ceramic tool for grinding herbs, minerals, and crystals.
- **Glass Vials**: Several sealable bottles for storing finished potions, poisons, or elixirs.
- **Measuring Cup**: A small, precise cup for measuring liquid ingredients.
- **Test Tubes and Rack**: For mixing and testing alchemical substances, including a rack for safe storage.
- **Basic Ingredients**: A selection of base ingredients such as mercury, saltpeter, and dried medicinal plants for common alchemical mixtures.
- **Goggles and Mask**: Protects against fumes and splashes when producing dangerous substances.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Alchemy checks
