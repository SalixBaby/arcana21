> _The **Hunting Toolkit** provides everything an experienced wildlife tracker or trapper needs. It assists with tracking, setting traps, and processing hunted prey._

The toolkit contains:

- **Folding Hunting Knife**: A sturdy tool for hunting and gutting.
- **Trap Building Kit**: Components for simple animal traps (e.g. snare, spring mechanism).
- **Animal Fat Soap**: For removing scent traces.
- **Wildlife Marker**: Ink and ribbons for marking trails or territories.
- **Camouflage Veil**: A mesh fabric for basic concealment in the wilderness.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Survival checks
