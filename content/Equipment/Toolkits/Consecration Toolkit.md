> _The **Consecration Toolkit** contains ritual and sacred objects such as those needed by priests, cultists, or holy order members for blessings, ceremonies, or defense against the unholy._

The toolkit contains:

- **Vial of Holy Water**: For blessing or as a weapon against the undead.
- **Ritual Candles**: In various colors, for symbolic purposes.
- **Symbol of Faith**: An amulet, pendant, or small shrine.
- **Pure Cloth**: For altars, the wounded, or anointings.
- **Prayer Scroll**: Containing sacred verses, blessing formulas, or banishment incantations.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Religion checks
