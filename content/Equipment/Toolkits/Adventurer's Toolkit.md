> _The **Adventurer's Toolkit** is the indispensable companion for all those who explore unknown places, traverse dangerous ruins, or embark on long journeys. It combines practical equipment for survival and exploration in the wilderness or underground complexes._

The toolkit contains:

- **Waterproof Map**: A writable map with a blank grid for personal entries.
- **Rope and Hook**: For climbing, securing, or improvised trap construction.
- **Travel Rations**: Durable provisions for several days.
- **Whetstone and Flint**: For maintaining weapons and starting fires.
- **Pocket Compass**: A simple navigation aid in unfamiliar terrain.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Climbing checks
