> _The **Cooking Toolkit** is the ideal toolkit for traveling cooks and adventurers who do not want to go without a good meal on long journeys. It contains the basic tools for preparing simple and delicious dishes._

The toolkit contains:

- **Small Cooking Knife**: Sharp and handy, for all kinds of preparation.
- **Travel Pan**: A lightweight, sturdy pan that can be heated over a fire.
- **Wooden Spatula and Tongs**: For cooking and turning food.
- **Spice Container**: Small tins for salt, pepper, and dried herbs.
- **Foldable Kettle**: For hot drinks or boiling water.

If you are proficient with this toolkit you additionally receive:

- Bonus on all Cooking checks
