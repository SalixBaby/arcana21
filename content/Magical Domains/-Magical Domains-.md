>***Magical Domains in Arcana21** represent the various types of magical forces that heroes can harness to shape the world and influence their opponents. These domains are divided into six major categories that reflect the breadth of magical possibilities. Each Magical Domain allows heroes to access specific elemental or metaphysical forces, which can often be further specialized. For example, the Water domain can also encompass poison, ice, or acid. Through the freedom to shape spells individually, Magical Domains become a flexible tool in the arsenal of heroes.*

>⚠️ **Note:** Even those who do not command magic themselves may find knowledge of the various Magical Domains important in certain situations.

#### The Categories:

##### **1. Body**
Body magic alters form, perception, and matter: [[./Body/Illusion|Illusion]], [[./Body/Senses|Senses]], [[./Body/Summoning|Summoning]], [[./Body/Transformation|Transformation]]
It allows the visible and tangible to be changed – from the transformation of a body to the creation of tangible illusions or new forms.
This magic transcends the boundaries of the physical and shapes the world according to the will of its practitioner.

---

##### **2. Elemental**
Elemental magic encompasses the primal forces of nature: [[./Elemental/Air|Air]], [[./Elemental/Earth|Earth]], [[./Elemental/Fire|Fire]], [[./Elemental/Water|Water]]
These spells are tangible, powerful, and often destructive – conjuring flames, storms, waves, or tremors. Yet elemental magic can be equally creative: shaping landscapes, altering materials, or harnessing natural forces such as **poison, ice, or gravity**.
Those who master the elements command the very foundation of the world.

---

##### **3. Mind**
The magic of the Mind opens access to intellect, emotion, and consciousness: [[./Mind/Communication|Communication]], [[./Mind/Dream|Dream]], [[./Mind/Manipulation|Manipulation]], [[./Mind/Mental|Mental]]
These spells penetrate the minds of others, influence thoughts, direct emotions, or create visions.
Mind mages understand that power is not always loud – sometimes a whisper in the minds of others is enough.

---

##### **4. Necromancy**
[[./Necromancy/Necromancy|Necromancy]] concerns itself with death, decay, and that which lies beyond life.
Its spells call forth the essences of the deceased, animate remains, or reveal the secrets of the world between worlds. This power is dangerous, seductive, and feared by many – those who wield it risk being touched by darkness themselves.

>⚠️ **Note:**
>**Necromancy** cannot be freely chosen. It is only obtained through **special events, rare abilities, or dark pacts**.

---

##### **5. Power**
Power magic concerns itself with the invisible principles that hold the universe together: [[./Power/Darkness|Darkness]], [[./Power/Light|Light]], [[./Power/Space|Space]], [[./Power/Time|Time]]
Its spells manipulate reality itself – illuminating shadows, distorting distances, stretching moments, or tearing portals between locations.
This magic is abstract and potent, shaping the laws of the world rather than following them.

---

##### **6. Soul**
Soul magic acts upon that which connects life and fate: [[./Soul/Blessing|Blessing]], [[./Soul/Curse|Curse]], [[./Soul/Healing|Healing]], [[./Soul/Protection|Protection]]
It strengthens the life force, blesses allies, curses enemies, or weaves shields from pure spiritual energy.
Soul mages touch the invisible – that which burns within every being, yet few can grasp.
