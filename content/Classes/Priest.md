>_You serve a higher power – whether a god, a spiritual principle, or the light itself. While others cast spells to unleash power, you channel your magic to protect, heal, and bless. You are the spiritual backbone of your companions, a beacon in the darkness, and a speaker for the good. Your strength is not a weapon, but a gift – one you know how to wield wisely, yet with purpose._

- **Class Category:** Spellcasting Class
- **Class Ability:** Silent Prayer
    - You can cast spells without the use of your hands or voice. Additionally, your overloaded healing and protection spells cannot be prevented by mortals.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Herald**: You are the voice of a higher power. You proclaim will, warning, and promise, speak prayers like commands and words like truths.
	- +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Perception|Perception]]
	- +1 [[../Attributes/Soul/Insight|Insight]]
	- Knowledge and double bonus in Religion/Myths
- **Radiant**: You need no mandate, no holy scriptures – your faith is goodness itself. You strive to bring healing and hope.
	- +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]
	- +1 [[../Attributes/Soul/Persuasion|Persuasion]]
	- Knowledge and double bonus in Religion/Myths

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Blessing of Healing**: Pure goodness flows through you. In addition to your known Magical Domains you receive the Healing Magical Domain.
- **Blessing of Rest**: A prayer during times of rest can work true wonders. During a Short Rest you can allow up to (Level x) allies to restore one additional resource.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Blessing of Restoration:** When you stabilize or heal someone, you can simultaneously remove one negative status effect known to you (except Exhaustion).
- **Blessing of Cleansing**: You can once attempt to cleanse a creature or location of corruption, disease, or curse. This requires a one-minute rite and a Focus check.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Blessing of Revival**: Once per day for 4 AP you can attempt to revive a deceased creature. The longer ago the death occurred, the more difficult the Focus check required.
- **Blessing of Silence**: Once per day for 1 AP you can create an aura of (Level x) 5 meters that dampens external magical influences for one minute. Within this aura opponents receive a penalty on spells, however a Focus check must be passed for each weakened spell.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Blessing of Combat**: In a fight for life and death, all allies within a radius of 5 meters regenerate one resource of their choice (Mana, Endurance, Inspiration, Health) at the start of their turn.
- **Blessing of Life**: Once per day outside of combat you can place a blessing on an ally that protects them from death once. If the ally would fall to 0 or fewer Health, they fall to 1 Health instead. The blessing lasts until you grant it anew.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Blessing of Strength**: Your mere presence fills allies with courage and resolve, while causing enemies to waver. All allies within a radius of 20 meters receive a bonus on their checks; all opponents within the same radius suffer a penalty.
- **Blessing of Miracles**: Once per week you can beseech a divine miracle – an extraordinary event that far surpasses ordinary magic. Whether rain in a drought, the revelation of a hidden path, or a heavenly apparition: the effect is unique and is determined together with the GM.
