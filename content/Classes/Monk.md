>_You are body, mind, and soul in perfect harmony. In silent temples, on wind-swept mountains, or amidst the turmoil of war, you have mastered yourself. Where others grasp for power, you seek inner balance – and find within it a strength greater than any weapon. Your movements are precise, your thoughts clear, and your strikes penetrate not only flesh, but the very essence of your enemies._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Ki
	- Your inner energy flows through you like an invisible current, and through years of training you have learned to direct this force with purpose.

- ***Additional explanation for Ki:***
	- ***Ki Points:** You receive a number of 2 + (Level x) 2 Ki Points, which regenerate during a Long Rest. During a Short Rest you regain (Level x) Ki Points. You can spend Ki Points a maximum of once per round.*
	- ***Effect:** You can use your Ki Points flexibly: for each point spent you can either regenerate 1 Endurance Point or enhance one of your class abilities.*

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Hermit**: You have withdrawn from the world to perfect body and mind. In silence you have learned to master yourself.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Constitution|Constitution]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]
    - Knowledge in Medicine/Anatomy
- **Pilgrim**: You are constantly on the move – not out of restlessness, but out of conviction. Every path is a trial, every encounter a lesson.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Perception|Perception]]
    - Knowledge in Survival/Orientation

#### **1st Level:**

- **Ki Breathing**: You have mastered a breathing technique that stabilizes you mentally and physically. Once per day you can center yourself in a moment of calm and ignore one negative status effect (except Exhaustion).
  *(Ki Enhancement: Ki Breathing costs 1 AP and can be used in combat.)*
- **Unarmed Mastery**: You are dangerous even without weapons. With bare fists you count as armed and can deal normal damage with them.
  *(Ki Enhancement: Your unarmed attack also pushes the target back (Level x) tiles.)*

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Redirect Energy**: When someone misses you with a physical attack you can spend 1 AP as a reaction to redirect that attack toward someone else within one tile. Whether it hits is decided by an attack check.
  *(Ki Enhancement: You can use this ability without paying its AP cost and receive a bonus on the attack check.)*
- **Paralyzing Strike**: You channel your inner energy into a targeted hit that disrupts your opponent's nervous system. For 1 AP, when attacking unarmed, you can inflict two Stun on your opponent.
  *(Ki Enhancement: Inflict one additional Stun on your opponent.)*

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Momentum**: Keep moving at all times. As long as you have used a movement action in the same round you receive a bonus on your attack checks.
- **Burst**: You can accelerate your movements to superhuman speed for a moment. Once per day you can double your Movement for one round.
  *(Ki Enhancement: You can use your Burst multiple times per day and run along walls during it, as long as you are back on the ground by the end of your turn.)*

#### **4th Level:**

- **Inner Flame**: When you are reduced to 0 Health in combat, you can once per day gather your remaining strength and gain 2 additional AP. These must be used on your next turn.
  *(Ki Enhancement: You can use Inner Flame multiple times per day and gain one additional AP for the next turn.)*
- **Strike Barrage**: For 4 AP you can perform a chain of unarmed attacks. After each successful hit you may immediately perform another unarmed attack. The chain ends as soon as an attack misses.
  *(Ki Enhancement: You receive a bonus on each of these attacks.)*

#### **5th Level:**

- **Equilibrium**: Your mind is clear, your body mastered. You have reached the goal of your journey. You are immune to mental magic and all potential damage is reduced by 1.
- **Immortal Spirit**: You age very slowly, require almost no sleep or food, and are immune to diseases and poisons – your mind and body are unshakeable.
