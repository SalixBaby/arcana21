>_You do not fight with muscle, but with mind. For you every battlefield is a chessboard, every move carefully considered, every command well thought out. Whether in the field, in ambush, or in the halls of warlords – your strength lies in the overview. You coordinate, direct, and manipulate the flow of combat until victory is inevitable. You are no lone warrior – you turn groups into a fighting force._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Warfare
	- Your presence on the battlefield changes everything – even before the first blow falls. You read the situation, assess the terrain, and issue precise commands that grant your allies a decisive advantage.

- ***Additional explanation for Warfare:***
	- At the start of each combat you may choose one of six tactical directives that benefits all allies within hearing range until the end of the combat:
		- **Swift Advance** – All allies receive **+1 AP** in the first round.
		- **Mobile Formation** – All allies receive **+1 to Movement**.
		- **Iron Morale** – All allies receive **+1 to Willpower**.
		- **Vigilant Stance** – All allies receive **+1 to Dodge**.
		- **Combat Boost** – All allies receive **+1 on attack checks**.
		- **Endurance** – All allies receive **+1 temporary Health** for this combat.

#### **Variations:**

- **Practitioner**: You have fought yourself, shouted commands, and made decisions under mortal peril. You trust your experience and react quickly when others are still deliberating.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Constitution|Constitution]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Soul/Persuasion|Persuasion]]
    - Knowledge and bonus in Military/Tactics & Survival/Orientation
- **Theorist**: You have devoured countless books, analyzed strategies, and thought through tactics in minute detail. Your mind is your greatest tool, and you prefer to act with foresight rather than instinct.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Knowledge|Knowledge]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Perception|Perception]]

#### **1st Level:**

- **Tactical Understanding**: You can intuitively understand any map or tactical document you lay eyes on and redraw it from memory.
- **Tactical Analysis**: You can spend one round analyzing the battlefield to discover a tactical advantage that benefits you and your allies.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Tactical Command**: Once per round you can spend 2 AP to transfer 1 AP to all allies within hearing range for their next turn. These can only be used for movement actions (e.g. Running, Jumping, Flying, etc.)
- **Tactical Retreat**: You recognize when a fight can no longer be won. While you and your allies are withdrawing or attempting to flee from a combat, all receive a bonus to Movement and Dodge.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Advanced Warfare**: Your benefits from Warfare apply twice. Additionally you can now issue up to two diffrent tactical directives per combat.
- **Tactical Advantage**: At the start of the second round of a combat you can once swap the initiative of two allies. You can also let yourself or other allies fall back in the order to prepare coordinated attacks.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Tactical Overview**: As long as you have not taken damage this round and are not immediately adjacent to an opponent, you gain 1 additional AP.
- **Tactical Support**: When you support an ally through a command or the Help action, they receive 1 additional AP for their next turn.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Master Warfare**: All your tactical directives are permanently active for you and all your allies.
- **Master Strategist**: Your tactical abilities are legendary. With enough time and resources you can work out your plans to the point where they are virtually infallible.
