>_You are no simple servant of your god – you are their sword, their shield, and their avenging hand. While others preach and pray, you march into battle to enforce the will of your faith with fire and steel. Whether as a fanatical inquisitor, holy warrior, or relentless enforcer of a higher power – you are not merely a believer, you are a living weapon, forged in the name of your god._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Divine Smite
	- You can channel the power of your faith twice per day. On a melee attack you can deal up to (Level x) additional holy damage. You can only use this ability against an opponent who has acted against the interests of your god.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Inquisitor**: No transgressor escapes your gaze, no lie outlasts your judgment. You hunt heretics and apostates with fanatical zeal, employing your divine powers to pass sentence on enemies.
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Body/Instinct|Instinct]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Strength|Strength]]
    - Knowledge in Religion/Myths
- **Templar**: You are the unyielding shield of your faith, a bulwark against heresy and chaos. Your power protects allies and grants you superhuman steadfastness.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Constitution|Constitution]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Soul/Persuasion|Persuasion]]
    - Knowledge in Religion/Myths

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Divine Insight**: Your god grants you visions or inspirations. Once per day you can attempt to receive a divine revelation, granting you a cryptic but potentially helpful insight into a current challenge.
- **Divine Presence**: Once per day you can amplify your presence so that it is perceived by others. You receive a bonus on Intimidation or Persuasion checks for one conversation.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Divine Judgment**: Once per day you can touch an opponent and for 1 AP place a holy judgment upon them. The target receives a penalty on all checks for the next round.
- **Divine Healing**: You can use your divine connection to heal injuries. Twice per day you can heal yourself for 2 AP by (Level x) Health.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Divine Power**: You can enhance your divine abilities and use up to 4 additional AP per combat, which must be part of an attack.
- **Divine Shield**: Once per day you can completely negate a single instance of damage against you as a reaction. This costs 4 AP however.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Divine Wrath**: When you strike down an opponent with a Divine Strike you can perform one additional Divine Strike in this combat.
- **Divine Weapons**: Your weapons are permanently imbued with divine power, granting you a permanent +1 holy damage on all attacks.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Divine Form**: You can assume the form of an avatar of your god for one minute. In this form you take no damage, gain 50 tiles of flight range, and all your attacks count as Divine Strikes.
- **Divine Gift**: Your god has chosen you to defend their faith. And to do so eternally. As long as you conduct yourself in accordance with your faith you do not age and are immune to diseases and minor injuries.
