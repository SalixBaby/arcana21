>_When others flee, you charge forward. In your heart burns a fire that knows neither reason nor pain. You are raw power, unleashed rage, and untamed will. Where others rely on strategy, you answer with pure instinct. You are storm, tremor, and beast in one – a warrior who has made their wrath into a weapon._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Frenzy
    - You can enter a state of frenzy (Level x) times per day for 1 AP. While in frenzy you ignore exhaustion, pain, and fear. You cannot be restrained and receive a bonus on all attack checks. The frenzy lasts as long as you pay 1 AP at the start of your turn to keep it going, but for a maximum of one minute.

---

### **Variations:**

- **Berserker**: An insatiable fire burns within you. When you enter frenzy, only the fight matters. Pain means nothing to you, reason yields to instinct. You are storm and steel – a living weapon.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Instinct|Instinct]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Strength|Strength]]
- **Host**: Something sleeps within you – another being that breathes through you. Its power flows through you in moments of rage. You are not alone in your body, and sometimes your boundaries blur.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Instinct|Instinct]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Knowledge|Knowledge]]
    - Knowledge in Religion/Myths

---

### **1st Level:**

- **Hunch:** You instinctively sense how dangerous your opponent is. At a glance you can tell whether they are physically superior, equal, or inferior to you.
- **Tough**: You are more resilient than others. Opponents receive a penalty on checks to physically influence you.

### **2nd Level:**

- **Reckless**: You can attack with full force, receiving a bonus on your melee attack checks for this turn. Until your next turn, however, opponents also receive a bonus on attacks against you.
- **War Cry:** Once per combat you can let out a mighty war cry that inflicts fear on opponents within (Level x) 5 meters (penalty on all checks for 1 round).

### **3rd Level:**

- **Combat Rush:** As long as your current Health is below half your maximum, you deal +1 additional damage with all attacks.
- **Bloodthirst:** Whenever you land a critical hit on an opponent, you can restore 1 Health.

### **4th Level:**

- **Charge:** As long as you are moving in a direct path toward an opponent you can see, you receive no repeat penalty while running or jumping.
- **Overwhelming Force:** No one can escape you. You receive a double bonus on grappling, immobilization, and shoving checks.

### **5th Level:

- **Unstoppable:** While in frenzy, neither magical nor non-magical effects can slow, push back, or control you, and you cannot fall below 1 Health.
- **Bloodlust**: While in frenzy, every time you kill an opponent you regain 2 Health and 1 Endurance Point.
