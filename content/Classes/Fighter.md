>_You are the embodiment of physical discipline and martial art. While others rely on magic or tricks, you trust in training, willpower, and sheer determination. You are a master of the weapon, a bulwark of steel and resolve, a rock in the tide of chaos. Your strength lies not only in your power, but in your ability to endure, to strike – and to never give up._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Warrior's Heart
    - Your body is your weapon. You gain an additional (Level x) 2 Endurance Points and are proficient with all common weapons. You can also carry up to four weapons on your person simultaneously and switch between them flexibly in combat.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Gladiator**: Your fight is a show – and you are the star. You fight for the audience, for recognition, for applause. And you hit just as hard as anyone else – only with style.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Soul/Performance|Performance]]
    - Knowledge in **Military/Tactics** & **Art/Culture**
- **Soldier**: You learned on the battlefield, not in training halls. Your movements are efficient, your posture disciplined, your gaze cold. You do not fight for glory – but because it must be done.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Constitution|Constitution]]
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Strength|Strength]]
    - Knowledge in **Military/Tactics** & **Survival/Orientation**

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Weapon Mastery**: Choose one of the 21 weapon types. You receive a bonus on attacks with that weapon type.
- **Weapon Warden**: You can repair, sharpen, or adjust almost any weapon or piece of armor. After a short rest you can grant an ally a bonus on their first attack check with a specific weapon.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Resilience**: You can spend 1 Endurance Point (Level x) times per day to completely negate 1 damage – including from magical attacks.
- **Death Struggle**: When you are on deaths door, you can continue to use your Endurance Points. You also receive a bonus on the Constitution check to stay alive.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Pain Tolerance**: You can suppress pain like almost no other. During medical procedures, against poisons, or under torture you receive no penalty on checks.
- **Combat Rush**: As long as you are in combat and neither fleeing nor hiding, you receive +1 on your attack checks for each round the combat has lasted.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Unyielding**: You can spend up to 3 Endurance Points at once on your turn.
- **Iron Will**: You can resist control, fear, or mental effects with a check, even when you would normally not be permitted to make one.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Combat Master**: You are a true master of armed combat – every movement is precise, every stance carries weight. Even in failure there is still danger in you: your attacks always deal at least 1 damage, even if the attack misses its target.
- **Iron Stamina**: Through years of discipline, hardship, and training you have pushed your body beyond its natural limits. Your supply of Endurance Points is inexhaustible.
