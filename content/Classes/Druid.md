>_As a **Druid**, you are a guardian of nature, deeply connected to the balance of the world. Whether dense forests, vast steppes, or dark swamps – you understand the language of the wilderness and live in harmony with the forces of the earth. Your magic springs from the natural flow of life, and you can adapt to animals or plants to harness their strengths. In nature you are unstoppable, and even in cities you sense its hidden roots._

- **Class Category:** Spellcasting Class
- **Class Ability:** Shapeshifting
    - Your connection to nature allows you to alter your own form – from subtle adaptations to a complete transformation into a powerful creature.
    
- ***Additional explanation for Shapeshifting:***
	- ***Cosmetic Changes (0 Mana):** Without the use of Mana you can make small changes to your body that provide no real mechanical advantage but influence your appearance – for example growing whiskers, covering your skin with moss, or making your eyes glow.*
	- ***Partial Transformation (1 Mana):** With the use of 1 Mana you can adapt certain physical features that grant you a situational advantage. For example, you can take on the eyes of an eagle for better sight, the arms of a gorilla for greater strength, or a hand of vines for climbing.*
	- ***Full Transformation (2+ Mana):** Only with a minimum expenditure of 2 Mana can you fully transform into another creature. The more Mana you invest, the more powerful the form you assume. While transformed you gain additional Health equal to the Mana spent (no limit). The transformation lasts for (Level x) 10 minutes or ends as soon as your additional Health is depleted. While fully transformed you cannot cast spells, as your new form restricts your magical control.*

#### **Variations:**

- **Animal Focus**: Your connection to animals is instinctive, and they see you as one of their own. You think, act, and react with the clarity of a predator – alert, focused, and untamed.
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Body/Instinct|Instinct]]
    - Knowledge and bonus in Nature/Creatures and Survival/Orientation
    - Shapeshifting into animal form
- **Plant Focus**: You are one with the calm, unwavering power of the plant kingdom. You hear the whisper of ancient trees, feel the growth of young shoots, and understand how life itself continues through roots and spores.
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Mind/Experience|Experience]]
    - Knowledge and bonus in Nature/Creatures and Survival/Orientation
    - Shapeshifting into plant or fungal form

#### **1st Level:**

- **Call of Nature**: You can find simple medicinal herbs or edible plants in the wilderness without making checks. If you remain in one location for 24 hours you can learn information about the nature of that place.
- **Soul Companion**: Your deep connection to nature manifests in a faithful companion. Depending on your variation this can be a small animal or a living plant. This should be agreed upon with the GM.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Natural Healing**: You can treat wounds with herbs from nature. Once per day you can heal someone outside of combat by (Level) Health without using Mana or healing potions.
- **Nature Attuned**: You receive a bonus to Instinct, Experience, and Intuition while in the wilderness.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Elemental Form**: You are one with the elements. Through your Shapeshifting you can additionally transform into elemental beings whose Magical Domain you command.
- **Nature Magic**: As long as you are in nature, you receive a bonus on your Arcana checks.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Wild Regeneration**: When you fall below half of your Health, you automatically heal 1 Health at the end of your turn. This is triggered only once per day and stops when you either suffer a critically hit or return to full Health.
- **Timeless Guardian**: Nature grants you a timeless body as thanks for your service. For every year that passes, your body ages by only one month.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Legendary Form**: You have perfected Shapeshifting. Once per week you can transform into legendary creatures (e.g. a dragon or an Ent). This transformation lasts for up to one minute and grants you immense power.
- **Nature Mastery**: You control nature in its purest form. Once per week you can cast a particularly powerful nature spell that, for example, summons a storm, creates a forest, or heals an entire area.
