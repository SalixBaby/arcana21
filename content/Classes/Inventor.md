>_You are a tinkerer, a visionary, a master of mechanics and innovation. While others rely on magic or raw strength, you depend on your mind, your tools, and your tireless creativity. With skilled hands and a brilliant intellect you create mechanical marvels, ingenious devices, and deadly constructions. Whether sophisticated weapons, useful contraptions, or autonomous machines – your inventions are your greatest asset._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Contraptions
	- You can build one small, useful contraption per day. Contraptions can grant you bonuses on checks or produce other effects. You can keep (Level x2) contraptions functional simultaneously. The functions of all contraptions should be agreed upon with the GM.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Hextech**: You combine external magic with technology, creating artifacts that function both mechanically and magically.
    - Knowledge and bonus in Mathematics/Engineering or Craftsmanship/Metallurgy
    - +2 Magical Domains
    - Proficiency with [[../Equipment/Toolkits/Tinkerer's Toolkit|Tinkerer's Toolkit]]
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Mind/Arcana|Arcana]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]
- **Tinkerer**: You focus on creating practical, mechanical solutions to problems and challenges, from small contraptions to complex machines.
    - Knowledge and bonus in Mathematics/Engineering or Craftsmanship/Metallurgy
    - Proficiency with [[../Equipment/Toolkits/Tinkerer's Toolkit|Tinkerer's Toolkit]]
    - Access to firearms (should be agreed upon with the GM)
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Mechanical Precision**: You receive a bonus on checks requiring dexterity and mechanics, such as deactivating traps or assembling complex machines under time pressure.
- **Automaton Companion:** You can create a small mechanical companion that assists you. Depending on its design it can deliver messages, carry out small tasks, or even provide support in combat.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Logical Thinking**: You always have a plan. (Level x) times per day you can help your allies without being talented in that area. This must be declared in advance.
- **Gunpowder Mage**: You can incorporate gunpowder into your contraptions to make them explode at the right moment. This deals 1 damage to creatures and significantly more to structures.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Emergency Mechanism:** Once per day a contraption can activate itself to protect you. It responds to a trigger of your choosing and grants you a bonus on a check or passive value.
- **Combinable**: You can combine two small contraptions into a larger construct. These can take on more complex tasks, deal more damage, or prevent it.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Construct Armor**: You can use constructs to function as armor. These grant the wearer a bonus to Dodge and protection against 1 physical damage. They must be maintained by you every few days, just like normal Heavy Armor.
- **Flash of Genius**: Your sharp mind allows you to reroll a failed check. You can activate this ability 2x per day with your own checks or checks of allies within sight or hearing range.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Masterful Invention**: You can combine 3 constructs to create a monumental machine or a masterpiece of technology. This could be a giant combat robot, a flying device, or a powerful defensive installation.
- **Autonomous Intelligence**: Your constructs can think and act independently. They are also capable of seizing control of other machines.
