>_You walk the threshold between life and death, power and sacrifice. Your strength does not spring from divine grace or disciplined study, but from your own blood. Where others depend on external sources of magic, you draw your power from your own body – and pay a heavy price for it. Whether dark rituals, cursed blades, or arcane knowledge that is taboo to others – you know no fear when it comes to the pursuit of power._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Blood Pact
    - You can sacrifice your own Health to gain strategic or combat advantages. Each time you sacrifice Health, you can convert it into another resource depending on your specialization. This can only be done once per round.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Vessel**: You use your own body as the source of your power. Blood, pain, and endurance are the fuel of your magic – each activation strengthens you, yet visibly drains your Health.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Constitution|Constitution]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Strength|Strength]]
    - Knowledge in Religion/Myths
    - **Blood Pact Manifestation** – _1 AP_: You can sacrifice 1 Health to receive a bonus on your next check.
- **Executioner**: Your presence heralds merciless determination. Each of your attacks carries the intensity of your own life energy, striking with a precision and force that shakes your opponent – as though your blood itself were guiding the blade.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Constitution|Constitution]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]]
    - Knowledge in Religion/Myths
    - **Blood Pact Manifestation** – _1 AP_: You can sacrifice 1 Health to deal 1 additional damage with your next attack.

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Mark of Blood:** You can perform a ritual using blood as a medium to gather information or follow a trail.
- **Forbidden Knowledge**: You receive a bonus on Knowledge checks relating to dark magic, rites, or similar subjects.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Cursed Strike**: Once per day you can perform a cursed strike that, in addition to your normal damage, places a curse on the target. The target suffers a penalty on all checks until your next turn.
- **Blood Bond**: Outside of combat you can perform a ritual and unite yourself with an ally in blood. You both always know where the other is and can once per day look through each other's eyes. This bond is unique and ends only with death.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Life Drain**: You extract the remaining life energy from a defeated opponent and convert it into your own strength. The ritual takes 10 minutes, kills the target permanently, and restores up to 5 Health to you.
- **Blood Sense**: You can perceive fresh blood within a radius of (Level x) 10 meters and determine whom it belongs to. You receive vague impressions of the creature and how it was injured.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Blood Consumption:** Each time you kill an opponent, you can pay 2 AP as a reaction and absorb a portion of their life force. You immediately restore 2 Health as a result.
- **Blood Surge**: When you are below 50% of your maximum Health you gain one additional AP per round.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Awakened Bloodline**: Your body has adapted to blood magic. Each time you sacrifice Health, you receive double the bonus.
- **Eye for an Eye**: Once per week you can hold a grand ritual in which you absorb the essence of willing creatures. For each willing soul that sacrifices itself for you your maximum Health and lifespan increase – albeit at a great cost.
