>*As an **Aristocrat**, you are a master of social finesse – whether through birth, education, or sheer determination. You move confidently in the upper circles, knowing the language of diplomacy, the rules of etiquette, and the silent games of power. Perhaps you descend from an ancient lineage, perhaps you have earned your rank yourself – what matters is that you understand how influence is created and how it is maintained. With charm, tact, and calculating presence you know how to guide people and shape situations to your advantage. Your reputation is your armor – and your word often more effective than any sword.*

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Reputation
	- Your name opens many doors. You can use your reputation to request an audience, a favor, or special treatment. Your reputation is increased through alliances, bonds, and fulfilled favors, and diminished through broken promises and improper etiquette.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Diplomat**: You are a master of negotiation and know how to resolve conflicts before they escalate.
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Soul/Persuasion|Persuasion]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Soul/Performance|Performance]]
    - Knowledge in History/Languages
    - +1 Language
- **Duelist**: You are a master of honorable combat and know how to resolve conflicts with skill and blade in single combat.
    - Bonus on attack rolls in one on one combat
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Soul/Performance|Performance]]
    - Knowledge in Military/Tactics

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Authority**: You can grant a bonus to one of your Soul checks (Level) times per day. This bonus can be stacked.
- **Inspiring Command**: Through skilled words and sovereign leadership you redirect the use of your energy to your team. Once per round you can transfer up to (Level x) of your remaining Action Points (AP) to one or more allies within sight and hearing range.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Intimidating Presence**: Your standing and bearing can unsettle opponents. They receive a penalty to their Willpower within a radius of (Level x) 10 meters around you.
- **Inspiring Presence**: Your bearing can help your allies achieve great things. They receive a bonus to their Willpower within a radius of (Level x) 10 meters around you.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Political Intrigue**: You know how to play people against each other to gain advantages for yourself. Once per combat you can attempt to incite a group of opponents against one another, causing them to attack each other or fall into dispute. (Check against Willpower)
- **Master of Etiquette**: You have mastered the art of courtesy and diplomacy. You receive a bonus on all checks involving polite or diplomatic conversations.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Forced Negotiation**: Your diplomatic abilities are so refined that you can even force short-term agreements with enemies. Once per day you can interrupt a combat action to conduct a brief negotiation, which can secure time or resources for you and your team.
- **Privileged Protection**: You receive protection from well-disposed guards or escorts, increasing your Dodge by +10 as long as you remain in a favorable territory and in the proximity of allies.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Power of Monarchs**: Your reputation and influence are so great that you can demand direct audiences with rulers or important figures. Once per week you can ask a powerful political figure for a special favor, granting you access to resources, armies, or knowledge.
- **Royal Decree**: You possess the charisma and persuasive power to force immediate decisions even in crisis situations. Once per day you can compel a group or institution to act on your command, even if this runs contrary to their own interests.
