>_You do not fear death – you rule over it. While others bow to the inevitable, you have found ways to circumvent, control, or even reverse it. Your magic penetrates the boundary between life and decay, and you walk paths that even among spellcasters are considered forbidden. Whether as a pragmatic corpse summoner, ominous death priest, or banished soul shaper – you speak with the beyond, and the beyond listens._

- **Class Category:** Spellcasting Class
- **Class Ability:** Necromancy
    - You command a forbidden form of magic that penetrates death itself. You can summon spirits, animate corpses, and bind souls. This domain stands outside the 20 natural Magical Domains – for it obeys only the rules of transience.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Gravedigger**: You walk the threshold between life and death. For you, graves are not endpoints but sources of knowledge and truth.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Experience|Experience]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Knowledge|Knowledge]]
    - Knowledge in Religion/Myths
- **Sculptor**: You see bodies not as sacred or corrupted, but as malleable material. Bones, flesh, and remains are for you raw materials from which something new can emerge.
	- +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]
	- +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]
	- Knowledge in Medicine/Anatomy

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Servants of the Grave:** You can summon (Level x) dead simultaneously for simple tasks. They can open doors, carry objects, or warn you of danger – but possess no free will.
- **Death Sense**: You can almost taste death. You are aware of every death of every creature within a radius of (Level x) 10 meters around you. You know how long ago the death occurred but not the cause.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Whispers from Beyond**: When you have killed an opponent you can bind a fragment of their soul to yourself. You can hold up to (Level x) fragments simultaneously and converse with them. These fragments can grant you bonuses in Knowledge categories that the GM deems appropriate.
- **Unholy Presence:** Your mere aura instills fear. Opponents within (Level x) 5 meters receive -1 on all checks.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Awakening:** You are able to bring back more powerful dead. To subject them to your will you must pass a successful Focus check – the stronger the creature, the greater the resistance. You can control up to two powerful Undead simultaneously in this way.
- **Cursed Gaze:** For 2 AP you can afflict an opponent within (Level x) 10 meters with a curse. That opponent receives a penalty on all checks until the end of their next round.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Soul Bond:** Once per week you can bind a willing soul to any dead body. This soul is not under your control.
- **Death Touch:** Your magic penetrates living bodies as well. Once per day you can touch a creature and deal (Level x) 2 damage to it. Undead are instead healed by the same amount.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Dark Binding:** Once per week you can attempt to reanimate a legendary creature (e.g. a dragon) as an Undead under your control. This requires a ritual lasting several hours and a suitable corpse. You can control a maximum of one legendary creature at a time.
- **Immortal Essence:** Death is not the end. At least not for you. Your body does not age, and diseases and exhaustion cannot affect you. When you die, you return after seven years at a place of significance – changed, but alive. Immortality has its price.
