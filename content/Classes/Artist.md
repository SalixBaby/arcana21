>*A brushstroke, a melody, a poetic word – your art is more than mere entertainment. It moves hearts, influences minds, and can even shape reality. You are not only a performer or craftsman, but a creator of emotions, illusions, and truths. Your works inspire, confuse, or manipulate – depending on what the world needs in the moment.*

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** **Magical Art**
    - Your art is not merely beautiful – it possesses a supernatural effect. Whether through music, painting, poetry, or another form of expression: you can use your art to influence the behavior of others, produce magical effects, or even foresee the future.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Virtuoso**: Your talent lies in performance – whether music, acting, or dance. Your performances can enchant, soothe, or stir.
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Soul/Performance|Performance]]
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Soul/Persuasion|Persuasion]]
    - Knowledge and double bonus in Art/Culture
- **Visionary**: Your art is not only emotional, but almost prophetic. With your paintings, sculptures, or writings you can capture visions or even glimpse into the future.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/Perception|Perception]]
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Soul/Performance|Performance]]
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]
    - Knowledge and double bonus in Art/Culture

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Inspiring Art**: You can use an art form (Level x) 2 times per day to grant allies within sight or hearing range a bonus on a check. This costs 1 AP and must be declared in advance.
- **Jack of All Trades**: You have acquired a broad range of skills and can find your footing in almost any situation. You receive +1 on all non-physical attributes in which you are not talented.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Gripping Performance:** Your words and performances capture the attention of your audience. You receive a bonus on Persuasion and Performance when telling a story, playing a song, or delivering a performance.
- **Forger**: You can create near-perfect copies of writings or other art forms as long as you have seen them with your own eyes.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Inspiring Performance:** When outside of combat you deliver a speech, play a song, or perform another artistic display, you can grant all allies within sight or hearing range two additional temporary Endurance Points for one minute.
- **Artful Distraction**: Once per day you can manipulate a situation so that all eyes are on you while your allies act. Your allies receive a double bonus on Stealth checks during your performance.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Masterful Technique:** You can create extraordinary works. Your creations have a notable value and can be sold for vast sums.
- **Captivating Art**: For 2 AP you can perform your art and capture the attention of all creatures within sight or hearing range. Before they can act, they must first tear themselves away from your work. How difficult this proves depends on the quality of your performance. (Performance check against the Willpower of the creatures)

#### **5th Level:**

- **Masterpiece**: You can once create such a perfect work of art that it has permanent effects on all who are exposed to it. Whether positive or negative is up to you.
- **Luminary**: Your reputation as the finest artist in your field precedes you. People recognize you and your art, opening many doors and increasing your number of allies.
