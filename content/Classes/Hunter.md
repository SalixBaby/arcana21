>_Patience, precision, and the art of survival – these are the weapons of a Hunter. While others fight with raw force or magic, you rely on your skill, your experience, and your knowledge of prey and environment. Whether hunting in the wilderness or tracking a target in the city – nothing escapes your sharp senses. You know the tracks of your prey, know where it hides, and strike when the moment is most favorable._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Nemesis
    - You have specialized your abilities to combat a specific type of opponent with extraordinary efficiency – whether out of personal vendetta, years of experience, or practical necessity.

- ***Additional explanation for Nemesis:***
	- ***Choice of Enemy:** Choose a creature type at character creation (e.g. Humanoids, Undead, Dragons, Beasts, Demons, Constructs, etc.). You can designate further types as your Nemesis over the course of the game, but this must be discussed with the GM.*
	- ***Effect:** When you fight or hunt a being of your chosen type, you receive a bonus on all attack checks, Perception checks, Instinct checks, and your Willpower.*

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Bounty Hunter**: You hunt not only monsters, but also people – and you let no prey escape. You are a master at tracking down targets, setting traps, and delivering them alive or dead.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Soul/Intuition|Intuition]]
    - Knowledge in Trade/Economics & Survival/Orientation
- **Ranger**: You are an experienced hunter who knows their environment like the back of their hand. Whether animal or monster – you know how to track your prey and bring it down.
    - +2 to [[../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]]
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Body/Instinct|Instinct]]
    - Knowledge in Nature/Creatures & Survival/Orientation

#### **1st Level:**

- **Animal Companion**: You can have an animal companion at your side. This animal can assist you in combat or support you during the hunt. This must be discussed with the GM.
- **Favored Terrain**: You grew up in a specific environment and have adapted exceptionally well to its conditions. Choose one category: Civilization, Forests, Mountains, Water, Desert. As long as you are in this area, you receive +1 on all checks.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Trap Setter**: You can build sophisticated traps to catch animals or enemies. These traps are particularly effective with sufficient prior knowledge of your prey and the terrain.
- **Mark**: You can pay 3 AP to mark an opponent with the unique sign of the Hunter. You receive a bonus on attack checks against the marked opponent. When you bring down a marked opponent you can transfer the Hunter's mark to an opponent within (Level x) 5 meters without paying the AP cost.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Snare Trap**: You can once per day deploy a specially prepared snare trap that immobilizes an opponent for one round after a successful hit. You can also attach the snare trap to projectiles so that it triggers on impact – however this must be declared before the attack.
- **Hunting Instinct**: You receive a permanent bonus on all Instinct, Experience, and Intuition checks.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Pattern Recognition**: Every missed attack contains information about your opponent's evasion pattern. After a missed attack against an opponent you receive a bonus on your next attack check against that opponent.
- **Terrain Master**: You can select one additional Favored Terrain. As long as you are in a Favored Terrain you additionally receive +1 on all checks.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Master Hunter**: You become the ultimate hunter. You can track down any creature, no matter how far away or how well hidden. You receive vague impressions of where your prey is located and receive an additional bonus on all search and hunt checks.
- **Sharpshooter**: Your skill with ranged weapons reaches its peak. If you can see it, you can hit it. As long as you can directly see your prey you receive no penalty for range or cover.
