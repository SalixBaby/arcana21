>*As an **Alchemist**, you master the art of herbalism and potion-crafting. You are capable of producing powerful potions and mixtures with healing or harmful effects. Your knowledge of nature allows you to achieve powerful results from simple ingredients. You are a master of the chemical and botanical sciences, and know how to use your surroundings to the advantage of your team or the weakening of your enemies.*

- **Class Category:** Spellcasting Class
- **Class Ability:** Elixir Brewer
	- You can prepare up to (Level + 1) spells daily in potion form, which remain potent for up to 24 hours. These may be overloaded and can also be used by others. You must record the effects of each potion in writing, and upon use an Arcana check determines how potent the remedy proves to be.

#### **1st Level:**

- **Ingredient Master**: You can find the right ingredients for your potions in almost any environment. You also gain detailed knowledge of chemistry, alchemy, and magical plants and materials, and are able to extract possible alchemical essences.
- **Experimentalist**: Once per day you can produce an experimental elixir whose effect is unpredictable but potentially powerful. Upon consumption, the GM may consult a chaos table that can trigger unforeseen magical effects.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Flask Throw**: You acquire a special contraption that allows you to throw small vials with precision, granting a bonus on all range attack checks.
- **Poison Master**: You can produce poisons and antidotes more rapidly, and increase the healing effect of potions against poisons and diseases. (Bonus on effect)

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Waste Not**: Should your Arcana check fail during brewing or throwing, a side effect is produced nonetheless. Your mixture does not dissipate uselessly – instead it generates either a small smoke cloud, a weak bang effect, or minimal damage. A failure is never entirely wasted.
- **Improved Extraction**: You can utilize ingredients more efficiently, producing stronger potions from less material. Your potions now remain potent for up to 48 hours and receive an additional bonus to their effects. You can also identify any potion you examine and subsequently reproduce it yourself.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Golden Touch**: You learn the art of the permanent conversion of materials. You can transform simple substances into valuable or useful materials to produce potions or generate profit.
- **Elixir of Life**: Once per day you can brew a powerful life elixir that, upon immediate consumption, grants complete healing and can stabilize a critical condition.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Master Alchemist**: You can produce up to 5 experimental elixirs per day, with the ability to control their effects in a targeted manner. Your potions are so potent that they can amplify effects or even become permanent in nature.
- **Philosopher's Stone**: You have attained the ability to create the legendary Philosopher's Stone. This allows you to produce potions that grant eternal youth, immortality, or extreme power – albeit at a great cost.
