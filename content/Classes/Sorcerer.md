>_Magic flows through your veins like blood – wild, luminous, alive. You are not a student of the arts, but their vessel. Where others memorize formulas, you channel the arcane currents with instinct and intellect. Some call you a conduit, a wonder, a threat – but you know: you are magic, born in living form._

- **Class Category:** Spellcasting Class
- **Class Ability:** Overstrain
	- Once per round you can push beyond your limits and cast a spell as though you had overloaded it with (Level x) Mana Points – without paying the Mana cost. However, the overload leaves its mark: you suffer one Exhaustion.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Chaosling**: Raw, untamed Arcana rages within you. Your spells flicker, swirl, and explode – sometimes overwhelming, sometimes unpredictable. Yet in the chaos you find possibilities that others would never dare to attempt.
    - +2 [[../Attributes/Mind/Arcana|Arcana]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Instinct|Instinct]]
    - -2 [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]
    - **Special:** With every overloaded spell the GM may consult a chaos table that can trigger unforeseen magical effects.
- **Magus**: Your magic follows principles, patterns, and ancient formulas. You strive for control, precision, and efficiency. No wild fire burns within you – but a steady light.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Experience|Experience]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Knowledge|Knowledge]]

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Arcane Sense:** You can sense when magic is cast or has been cast in your vicinity (Level x 10 m). An Arcana check determines how well you understand the spell and how far back you can see.
- **Arcane Power**: Pure arcane power flows through you. In addition to your known Magical Domains you receive one Magical Domain from the Power category.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Arcane Field:** Your presence amplifies the magic around you – you and all allies within a radius of (Level x) 5 meters receive +1 on their Arcana checks.
- **Arcane Insight**: Spells always leave their traces. You can attempt to determine which Magical Domain someone within (Level x) 5 meters possesses. This can only be used once per target, and on a failed check the target becomes aware of what you are attempting.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Arcane Echo:** When you have successfully understood a spell through your Arcane Sense, you can attempt to copy it. You can only attempt this once and only while you remain within range of the Arcane Echo.
- **Arcane Impulse:** When you successfully cast an overloaded spell, you gain 1 additional AP for that turn.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Arcane Ease**: When you cast multiple non-overloaded spells in one round, you can ignore the repeat penalty for them.
- **Arcane Survival:** While on deaths door you can cast overloaded spells for 1 AP.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Arcane Manifestation:** Once per week you can allow your body to be flooded with pure magic – you glow, spells cost no Mana, and you hover slightly above the ground. Each round you must pass an increasingly difficult Focus check to maintain this state.
- **Arcane Regeneration**: Magic is part of your very being. You automatically regenerate 1 Mana Point at the start of your turn.
