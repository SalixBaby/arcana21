>_As a **Thief**, you are a master of shadows, deception, and quick fingers. You move silently through the streets, knowing every shortcut and every hiding spot. Whether you are pilfering valuables, slipping past guards unnoticed, or freeing yourself from seemingly hopeless situations – your skill and instinct make you a born survivor. Yet not all thieves are simple pickpockets. Some are information brokers, con artists, or burglars who move in the highest circles without anyone knowing their true identity._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Quick Reflexes
    - You can perform the "Use" and "Withdraw" actions in combat without paying their AP costs.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Acrobat**: You are agile and move with impressive skill across rooftops, ropes, and narrow ledges.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]]
    - Knowledge in Survival/Orientation
- **Con Artist**: You are a master of deception and fast talking, always ready to outwit others.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]
    - Bonus on [[../Attributes/Soul/Deception|Deception]]
    - Knowledge in Trade/Economics

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Sleight of Hand**: Your hands are nimble and precise. You receive a bonus on checks to steal, pick locks, escape restraints, or perform similar tasks of dexterity.
- **Improviser**: You can put everyday objects to creative use. In combat you can use anything as a weapon and receive no penalty on attacks.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Slow and Steady**: You can pay 1 AP to grant yourself a bonus on your Stealth checks. This action is exempt from the repeat penalty.
- **Keen Eye**: You have an infallible sense for hidden things. You receive a double bonus on checks to find secret doors, concealed mechanisms, or hidden traps.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Quick Escape**: When you are hit by an opponent, you can once per combat immediately perform two movement actions without paying their AP costs.
- **Double Life**: You can maintain multiple identities that aid you in social circles or during flight. You can switch your identity without arousing suspicion and pass yourself off as someone else. You can maintain up to (Level x) identities. These must be planned and agreed upon with the GM.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Sure Footing**: You can move with lightning speed across facades, ropes, or other surfaces. Climbing costs you no additional movement, and your Movement can no longer be affected by terrain. You also receive a bonus to your Speed.
- **Self-Preservation**: You can use the "Duck" action once per round without paying its AP cost.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Master of Escape**: You are nearly impossible to hold. When you are bound, imprisoned, or surrounded by opponents, you can once per day immediately escape, no matter how hopeless the situation appears. You also receive a further bonus to Speed.
- **Plunderer**: When you hit an opponent in combat or approach someone undetected, you can steal any useful loot they carry on their person (e.g. keys, gold, a scroll) without needing to make a check.
