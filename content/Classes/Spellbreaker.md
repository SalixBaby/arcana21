>_You are the counterpart to magic – a living ward, a wall of will and steel. Where others unleash sorcery, you bring silence. You pierce illusions, tear apart arcane patterns, and resist even the darkest hexcraft. Some call you heretical, others a protector. But you know: without control, magic is a weapon – and you are the shield._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Antimagic
    - You are a walking disruption in the magical fabric. Through inner discipline and your unique connection to the flow of magic, you can deliberately drain it of energy, weaken spells, or break their effect.

- ***Additional explanation for Antimagic:***
	- ***Antimagic Points:** You receive a number of 2 + (Level x) 2 Antimagic Points, which regenerate during a Long Rest.
	- ***Effect:** When a spell is cast within a range of (Level x) 10 meters, you can spend 2 AP to reduce that spell by as many Mana Points as you wish to expend Antimagic Points. With this ability you can also completely negate Level 0 spells by expending one Antimagic Point.*

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Purifier**: You see magic as a blemish, a sickness of the world. Your actions are not born of hatred, but of conviction. Every spell that falls is for you an act of cleansing.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Soul/Intuition|Intuition]]
    - Knowledge and bonus in Survival/Orientation
- **Silencer**: You are the void between spell and effect. In your presence magic loses its voice, its form, its will. Where others fight against spells, you simply take away their ability to be cast at all.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Knowledge|Knowledge]]
    - Knowledge in History/Languages

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Disruption Sense:** You possess a keen awareness of magical disturbance. Within a radius of (Level x) 10 meters you perceive when magic is cast or a magical object is active. Once per day you can also scan the surroundings for past magical influences and sense all spells that have been cast there within the last (Level x) days.
- **Arcane Resistance:** All opponents receive a penalty on Arcana checks to afflict you with a spell (e.g. enchantments, curses).

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Bane Strike:** You can (Level x) times per day enhance a melee attack with an Antimagic Point. The hit can end an active magical effect on the target (e.g. invisibility, magical shields).
- **Arcane Disruption**: Your attacks tear at the essence of spellcasters. When you hit a magically gifted creature you deal +1 additional damage, as your power disturbs their arcane structures.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Arcane Absorption:** For each Mana Point negated through your Antimagic Points, you can use one additional AP on your next turn.
- **Disruption Field:** Within a radius of (Level x) meters around you, hostile spellcasters cannot maintain concentration on spells.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Antimagic Grip:** Once per day you can spend 2 AP and 2 Antimagic Points to touch an opponent and suppress their magical abilities for 1 round.
- **Disenchanted Mind:** You are immune to magical thought manipulation (e.g. fear or illusion), unless the effect originates from a deity or an ancient being.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Arcane Severance:** You can sever a being's connection to the Arcana, preventing them from ever casting spells again. This requires a ritual lasting over a minute, in which Willpower checks decide the outcome.
- **Arcane Null:** Once per day you can create an antimagic field of 100 meters for 10 minutes. Within this field no spells, magical items, or summonings function – neither those of your friends nor those of your enemies.
