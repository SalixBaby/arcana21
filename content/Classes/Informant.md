>*Knowledge is power – and you know more than most. Your eyes and ears are everywhere, your words are sharp as daggers, and you know how to obtain information that others would prefer to keep hidden. You deliver information, manipulate situations behind the scenes, and are able to build connections that remain invisible to others.*

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Information Network
    - You possess a far-reaching network of informants, traders, and debtors. With every favor, trade, or exchange your influence grows – and with it your access to rare secrets, valuable resources, and discreet support. Your network can spread rumors, gather information, or carry out covert assignments.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Detective**: You observe, analyze, and connect the dots. You see what others miss and recognize patterns where others see chaos. Your strength lies not in attacks – but in information.
	- Bonus on [[../Attributes/Mind/Experience|Experience]]
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/Knowledge|Knowledge]]
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/Perception|Perception]]
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Soul/Insight|Insight]]
- **Schemer**: You are no silent observer, but a string-puller. Your knowledge is a currency, and you know exactly when to deploy which piece of information.
	- +2 [[../Attributes/Soul/Deception|Deception]]
	- +2 [[../Attributes/Soul/Persuasion|Persuasion]]
	- Bonus on [[../Attributes/Soul/Intuition|Intuition]]
	- Knowledge in Politics/Diplomacy

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Judge of Character**: You have an infallible sense for the emotions and inner weaknesses of others. You instinctively know which social skill would work best on a given person.
- **Master Haggler**: Your experience in buying and selling information strengthens your talent for negotiation. You receive a bonus on all negotiation checks.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Spread Rumors**: Once per day you can deliberately plant a piece of information or spread a rumor. Depending on how believable it is and how large your network is, it can spread and have consequences within the coming hours or days.
- **Identify Weakness**: You can spend 2 AP to take a closer look at an opponent. You receive a bonus on all attacks against that target for the duration of the combat.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Contact Man**: Wherever you go – you know someone. In every new region or city you find a familiar face who can help you in a specific situation – whether with information, resources, or discreet support.
- **Human Lie Detector**: Misinformation and lies are your daily bread. You receive a double bonus on Insight checks to detect falsehoods.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Lucrative Business**: You have grown your network into a profitable organization, and this does not go unnoticed. Without any effort on your part, more and more people join your network and your profit grows along with it.
- **Confidential Documents**: You can manipulate documents to appear authentic or decipher genuine documents, even when they are written in code or encrypted. This encompasses letters, contracts, and official papers.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Puppetmaster**: You have cracked the code of humanoid psychology. Once per week you can use targeted words and deception to make a person or group trust you or act in your interest for a certain period of time.
- **Syndicate**: Your information network has reached a critical mass. Once per week you can use it to spread rumors that the population accepts as fact. You can discredit the reputation of individuals or, with the right rumor, even provoke wars.
