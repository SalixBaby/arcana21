>_In **Arcana21**, classes are the core of a character and define which abilities and talents they develop over the course of the game. Each class brings unique abilities, strengths, and weaknesses that determine how a character behaves in combat and when exploring the world. The 21 different classes are divided into two main categories: **Combat Classes** and **Spellcasting Classes**._

- **Combat Classes** specialize in physical confrontations, melee combat, and tactics.
- **Spellcasting Classes** command the magical arts and are focused on the manipulation of arcane energies.

---

### Combat Classes

- [[./Aristocrat|Aristocrat]] – Commands influence, education, and social power, using status, networks, and authority as tools.
- [[./Artist|Artist]] – Uses art, words, or music to influence emotions, imitate magic, and distort reality.
- [[./Assassin|Assassin]] – A silent killer, specialized in precise attacks, preparation, and the elimination of individual targets.
- [[./Barbarian|Barbarian]] – Fights with raw strength, instinct, and rage, unleashing their untamed nature in combat.
- [[./Cleric|Cleric]] – A consecrated warrior of their faith, channeling divine power to protect, judge, or lead.
- [[./Fighter|Fighter]] – A versatile frontline combatant who masterfully employs weapons, armor, and combat technique.
- [[./Guardian|Guardian]] – Protects allies and key positions, standing as a bulwark between danger and objective.
- [[./Hunter|Hunter]] – A specialized tracker who analyzes prey, exploits weaknesses, and survives in the wilderness.
- [[./Informant|Informant]] – Gathers, analyzes, and sells knowledge and secrets, influencing events from the background.
- [[./Inventor|Inventor]] – Combines intellect and technology to create contraptions, weapons, and unconventional solutions.
- [[./Monk|Monk]] – Uses body, mind, and discipline as a weapon, relying on inner balance rather than external means.
- [[./Spellbreaker|Spellbreaker]] – Specialized in recognizing, disrupting, and dismantling magical effects and spells.
- [[./Tactician|Tactician]] – Controls the battlefield through planning, commands, and shrewd decisions.
- [[./Thief|Thief]] – Master of stealth, locks, and quick decisions, who prefers to circumvent problems rather than confront them head-on.
- [[./Witcher|Witcher]] – Taps into forbidden or foreign powers, using their own body or blood as a source of supernatural strength.

### Spellcasting Classes

- [[./Alchemist|Alchemist]] – Uses potions, elixirs, and chemical reactions to produce effects ranging from healing to destruction.
- [[./Druid|Druid]] – A guardian of nature who directs plants, animals, and natural forces, acting in harmony with the wilderness.
- [[./Necromancer|Necromancer]] – Commands death, decay, and souls, moving dangerously close to forbidden powers.
- [[./Priest|Priest]] – A spiritual servant of higher powers who embodies rituals, faith, and divine order.
- [[./Sorcerer|Sorcerer]] – A born spellcaster who shapes magic through control or chaos.
- [[./Wizard|Wizard]] – A learned spellcaster who shapes magic through study, discipline, and control.
