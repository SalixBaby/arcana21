>_You are no prodigy – you are the result of discipline, study, and tireless dedication. While others feel magic within themselves, you have understood it. You think in formulas, runic circles, and arcane theorems. Your power lies not in feeling, but in knowledge. You did not inherit magic – you earned it._

- **Class Category:** Spellcasting Class
- **Class Ability:** Magical Archive
    - You possess a personal Archivarium – a magical artifact in which you preserve your accumulated knowledge of Magical Domains.

- ***Additional explanation for Magical Archive:***
	- ***Appearance:** What this archive looks like is entirely up to you: perhaps it is a floating spellbook, a glowing crystal, or a bundle of whispering scrolls that follows you.*
	- ***Effect:** You receive 3 additional Magical Domains, and once per week you can attempt to permanently store a new Magical Domain in your archive. What prerequisites must be met is decided by the GM – this could include the study of ancient texts, the observation of another spellcaster, or a special experience with magic itself. The threshold, however, is always very high.*

---

### **Variations:**

- **Arcanist**: You did not find your power, but acquired it – with ink, discipline, and years of dedication. You believe in structure, in predictability – and in the conviction that knowledge is the true key to power.
    - +2 [[../Attributes/Mind/Focus|Focus]]
    - +2 [[../Attributes/Mind/Knowledge|Knowledge]]
    - Knowledge in History/Languages & Mathematics/Engineering
- **Chronicler**: You do not chase after truth – you collect it. Your magic lives from what has been experienced, not from what has been studied – and you know that even the smallest fragment of history can hold great power.
    - +1 [[../Attributes/Mind/Arcana|Arcana]]
    - +2 [[../Attributes/Mind/Knowledge|Knowledge]]
    - Knowledge in History/Languages & Survival/Orientation

#### **1st Level:**

- **Magical Runology**: You can read, recognize, and interpret magical runes of all kinds. As long as they are based on Magical Domains known to you, you can precisely redraw or reconstruct them according to your abilities.
- **Magical Diligence**: When you cast a spell with one minute of preparation (e.g. through a ritual, meditation phase, or the drawing of a circle), you receive a bonus on the Arcana check.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Magical Catalyst**: When you cast a spell in the presence of a fitting material (e.g. flames for fire spells, water for ice spells), you receive +1 on the Arcana check. The material may be consumed in the process.
- **Magical Detection**: Through your deep understanding of arcane patterns you can examine magical energy in a targeted manner. You receive a bonus on checks to search for or identify magical effects.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Magical Analysis**: You can spend 1 AP as a reaction to analyze enemy spells as they are being cast. With a successful Arcana check you know their exact effect – or weakness. You receive a bonus on spell duels against that spell.
- **Magical Anchor**: You can equip a spell with a time-delayed trigger. You can decide whether the spell unfolds after seconds, minutes, or upon a specific stimulus (e.g. a sound, a movement, a magical aura). These last a maximum of 24 hours however.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Magical Concentration**: You can maintain two spells simultaneously. It requires only one AP and a Focus check per round. If you lose concentration both spells end.
- **Magical Barrage**: As long as you repeatedly cast non-overloaded spells at a target and pass the check, these spells cost you no AP and are exempt from the repeat penalty. The check becomes two points harder with each success.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Magical Mastery**: You have penetrated the arcane arts to their very core. All regular Magical Domains except one are now available to you, regardless of your previous selection.
- **Magical Ultimatum**: Your knowledge of magic is absolute. You can overload spells to any degree – even beyond the regular Mana limit. For spells with more than 5 Mana spent, a Focus check determines how precisely and in a controlled manner you unleash them. Failure does not mean collapse, but unforeseen consequences.
