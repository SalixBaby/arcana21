>_As an **Assassin**, you are a master of silent elimination, concealment, and strategic execution. You move unseen through shadows, patiently observe your prey, and strike with lethal precision. Your craft is a blend of patience, planning, and deadly efficiency. Whether as a spy, hired killer, or shadow warrior – you use cunning and speed to eliminate your opponents before they even realize you are there._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Deadly Focus
    - When you surprise an opponent or land a full hit or critical hit, you can deal 1 additional damage.

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Hitman**: You do not kill out of passion or necessity – you do it because it is your profession. Whether with dagger, poison, or a precise shot – your art is the clean kill.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]]
- **Surgeon**: You know the body better than anyone else. You know exactly where to cut – to paralyze, to kill, or simply to cause pain.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]
    - Knowledge and bonus in Medicine/Anatomy

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Silent**: You can move with exceptional quiet and receive a bonus on Stealth checks.
- **Surprise Attack**: When you deal damage to an opponent undetected, they receive a penalty on their next attack check.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Hidden Presence**: You can conceal yourself more easily in crowds or dark corners. Opponents receive a penalty to detect you. As long as you have successfully hidden and do not move, you cannot be detected by non-magical means.
- **Mimicry**: After a brief conversation or observed interaction, you can imitate the voice, gestures, and mannerisms of a person with deceptive authenticity.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Poisoned Blade**: For 1 AP you can once per day coat your weapon with a paralyzing poison. If you hit an opponent with it, they are paralyzed for one round and cannot move.
- **Fight or Flight**: When you take damage, you can, if possible, immediately perform a counterattack for 1 AP or use your Movement without provoking an opportunity attack.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Double Strike**: When you attack an opponent from ambush, you can immediately perform a second attack without paying AP. For both of these attacks your target counts as surprised.
- **Master of Deception**: You can conceal yourself masterfully, even in unfavorable situations. Once per combat you can immediately hide, even while being observed. Opponents must pass a Perception check to detect you.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Deadeye**: Your skill with lethal weapons reaches its peak. You receive a double bonus on all attack checks.
- **Master Assassin**: You can silently and instantly kill an opponent if they have not noticed you and their Health is below 8.
