>_As a **Guardian** you are the shield in the darkness, the eye in the storm, and the first line of defense. Your task is not victory, but protection – of people, places, or secrets. Whether you are guarding someone, searching for clues, or analyzing threats, your gaze is sharp and your will steadfast. You are no champion on the battlefield – you are the wall against which chaos is shattered._

- **Class Category:** Combat Class
- **Class Ability:** Downfall
	- The moment of falling is for you not an end, but a trigger. When hope fades and blood is spilled, a merciless determination ignites within you.

- _**Additional explanation for Downfall:**_
    - _**Awakened Will:** When an ally falls to 0 Health, you enter a state of fanatical resolve. As long as this state persists, you gain 2 additional AP per round._
    - _**Last Stand:** When you yourself fall to 0 Health, your will passes to your companions. All allies gain 2 additional AP per round as long as you are incapacitated._
    - _**Legacy of the Fallen:** Should you die permanently, your sacrifice echoes until the end of the combat. All allies receive a bonus on all checks, 2 additional AP per round and (Level x) temporary Health._

---

#### **Variations:**

- **Bodyguard**: Your mission: the protection of one person. You analyze instinctively and recognize threats before others even perceive them.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Speed|Speed]]
    - +2 to [[../Attributes/Mind/Perception|Perception]]
    - Knowledge in Medicine/Anatomy
- **Shepherd**: You protect not just anyone, but everyone and everything. You are simultaneously protector, judge, and regrettably sometimes executioner.
    - +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/Constitution|Constitution]]
    - +2 to [[../Attributes/Mind/Perception|Perception]]
    - Knowledge in Politics/Diplomacy

---

#### **1st Level:**

- **Danger Sense**: You can sense threats in advance. You receive a double bonus on your Initiative checks.
- **Protect**: When an ally directly beside you suffers physical damage, you can pay 1 AP as a reaction to take half (rounded up) of the damage yourself.

#### **2nd Level:**

- **Taunt**: Up to (Level x) times per day you can deliberately provoke an opponent within (Level x) 10 meters. If your check succeeds, the opponent briefly loses their composure: they suffer a penalty on all their checks for one round and will attack you at the next opportunity, situation permitting.
- **Interrogation Tactics**: You quickly recognize when someone is concealing something. You receive a bonus on checks to see through lies, apply pressure, or question someone in a targeted manner.

#### **3rd Level:**

- **Eye for Detail**: Nothing escapes your knowing gaze. You receive a double bonus on checks to examine objects or locations.
- **Standstill**: When you hit an opponent with an opportunity attack, that opponent can no longer Run, Fly, or Jump this round. You also receive a bonus on opportunity attacks and can hit opponents who use the Withdraw action.

#### **4th Level:**

- **Shield**: As long as you are beside an ally and concentrating on them, opponents can no longer physically attack that ally.
- **Vigilant Presence**: All your allies within a radius of (Level x) 5 meters receive a bonus on Initiative checks and their Dodge.

#### **5th Level:**

- **Fortress**: You are an insurmountable protective wall for your allies. All opponents who can focus on you do so in every combat.
- **A Life for a Life**: You are not sure how, but when a person near you dies, you can sacrifice your life to bring them back. Your death, however, is final.
