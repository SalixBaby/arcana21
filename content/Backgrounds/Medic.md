>_The **Medic** is an expert in healing and anatomy. These characters are skilled at treating wounds, curing diseases, and saving the lives of others._

**Possible Traits:** Knowledge of anatomy, healing arts, medicine

**Blessing: Medical Expertise**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]]
- Knowledge and bonus in Medicine/Anatomy
- Access to medical equipment
- Emergency Care: You receive an additional bonus on stabilization checks for yourself and others.

**Curse: Savior Complex**
- Tendency to help others at one's own expense
- Difficulty saying no
    - Optional: Self-sacrifice
