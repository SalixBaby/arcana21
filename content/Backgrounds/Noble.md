> ***Noble** describes someone born into a wealthy and influential family. Characters with this background have access to education, wealth, and social networks, but often also high expectations to fulfill. They are well-versed in etiquette and politics, and skilled at recognizing as well as spinning intrigue.*

**Possible Traits:** Diplomacy, influence, connections to important figures

**Blessing: Excellent Education**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]] or [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge in Politics/Diplomacy
- +1 Training Point

**Curse: Known Name**
- Traveling unrecognized is difficult
- Political games and rivals
    - Optional: Arrogance
