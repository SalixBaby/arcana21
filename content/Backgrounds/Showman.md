>_The **Showman** travels from place to place to entertain others. These characters are often eloquent speakers who know how to captivate an audience. They are charming and can use their talents to win the trust and sympathy of others._

**Possible Traits:** Performing arts, charisma, improvisation

**Blessing: Charismatic Presence**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge in Art/Culture
- Talent in [[../Attributes/Soul/Performance|Performance]]
- Knowledge of acting and music

**Curse: Restlessness**
- Constant need for variety
- Difficulty staying in one place for long
    - Optional: Susceptible to vices
