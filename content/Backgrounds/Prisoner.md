>_The **Prisoner** has spent time behind bars and been hardened by the harsh conditions of prison life. These characters are often suspicious, tough, and constantly alert to threats._

**Possible Traits:** Self-defense, improvisation, survival instinct

**Blessing: Resilience**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge in Survival/Orientation
- Basic knowledge of dungeons and prison customs

**Curse: Paranoia**
- Tendency to distrust everyone at first
- Difficulty fitting into social norms
    - Optional: Irritability
