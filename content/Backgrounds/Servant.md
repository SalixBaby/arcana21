>_The **Servant** has devoted their life to the service of others. They are loyal, hardworking, and skilled at fulfilling the wishes of their master. These characters are often discreet and inconspicuous, but also irreplaceable._

**Possible Traits:** Observation, patience, household management

**Blessing: Discretion**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge in Art/Culture
- Basic knowledge of various hierarchies and customs

**Curse: Submissive**
- Dislikes being alone
- Tends not to step outside their role
    - Optional: Low Self-Confidence
