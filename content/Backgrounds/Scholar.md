>*The **Scholar** has spent many years accumulating knowledge and studying complex subjects. They are often researchers, historians, or magic experts who are well-versed in their fields and have a deep understanding of books and ancient writings.*

**Possible Traits:** Knowledge advantage, research skills, access to archives and libraries.

**Blessing: Bookworm**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]]
- Knowledge in History/Languages
- Bonus on gathering information in libraries or archives
- +1 Training Point

**Curse: Thirst for Knowledge**
- Difficulty leaving secrets or questions unanswered
- Often overanalyzes situations
	- Optional: Coward
