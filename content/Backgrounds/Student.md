>_The **Student** is still in the process of learning and strives for knowledge and skills. These characters are curious and eager to learn, with the potential to develop and grow quickly._

**Possible Traits:** Curiosity, ability to learn, adaptability

**Blessing: Fast Learner**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]]
- Knowledge in Mathematics/Engineering
- Access to learning materials and a basic understanding of science
- +1 Training Point

**Curse: Inexperienced**
- Lack of experience can lead to mistakes
- Often dependent on mentors
    - Optional: Naivety
