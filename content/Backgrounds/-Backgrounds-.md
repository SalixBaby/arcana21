>*The **Backgrounds** in **Arcana21** are a central part of character creation and shape a hero's past, personality, and abilities. They not only provide insight into the character's history, but also grant special skills, connections, or advantages that can significantly influence their adventure. Whether descending from noble houses, shaped by harsh training, or strengthened through dark pacts, backgrounds allow players to give their character a deep, individual story.*

A background describes **where your character comes from and what shaped them**, long before the adventure began. Origin, upbringing, and earlier circumstances leave their mark – in knowledge, attitude, and instinctive decisions.

Choose carefully, for **your past determines how you face the world**, whom you trust, and which paths lie open to you.

[[./Athlete|Athlete]] - Your body was shaped by training, competition, or physical discipline. Performance, endurance, and self-control are the focus of your life.
[[./Craftsman|Craftsman]] - You have learned to create something lasting with your hands. Precision, patience, and knowledge of materials are your strengths.
[[./Cultist|Cultist]] - You were/are part of a sect, a circle, or a hidden faith. Secret knowledge, rituals, and questionable teachings still accompany you today.
[[./Faith|Faith]] - Your life is shaped by religion, spirituality, or divine order. You follow teachings, rituals, and moral principles.
[[./Farmer|Farmer]] - A life shaped by hard work, simple means, and the rhythm of nature. You know how to make do with little and endure.
[[./Gambler|Gambler]] - Luck, risk, and calculation drive your actions. You know when to bet, bluff, or fold.
[[./Medic|Medic]] - You have learned to preserve life or ease suffering. Anatomy, illness, and injuries are familiar to you.
[[./Merchant|Merchant]] - Trade, negotiation, and profit were your everyday life. You understand markets, people, and the value of things – and of information.
[[./Military|Military]] - Discipline, chains of command, and combat were part of your everyday life. You know order, strategy, and the gravity of armed conflict.
[[./Nature|Nature]] - The wilderness was your home or your teacher. You understand animals, plants, and the dangers beyond civilization.
[[./Noble|Noble]] - Raised amid privilege, intrigue, and power structures. You know etiquette, influence, and the rules of the ruling class.
[[./Showman|Showman]] - Your life took place on stages, in markets, or before an audience. Entertainment, attention, and adapting to crowds come naturally to you.
[[./Prisoner|Prisoner]] - Freedom was once taken from you – whether rightfully or wrongfully. This time hardened you and shaped your view of authority.
[[./Researcher|Researcher]] - Driven by curiosity and a thirst for knowledge, you have investigated places, phenomena, or ancient secrets.
[[./Sailor|Sailor]] - The sea was your workplace or home. You know storms, navigation, and life in close quarters.
[[./Scholar|Scholar]] - Years of study and theory have sharpened your mind. Books, archives, and discussions are more familiar to you than the battlefield.
[[./Servant|Servant]] - You have lived in the shadow of others and learned to observe rather than stand out. Obedience, discretion, and adaptability define you.
[[./Smuggler|Smuggler]] - You move goods, information, or people outside official channels. Secrecy, contacts, and risk are nothing new to you.
[[./Street Urchin|Street Urchin]] - You grew up without protection or structure. Survival, improvisation, and a keen sense for danger have shaped you.
[[./Student|Student]] - You are still in training or have recently completed it. Learning, imitation, and first steps of your own define you.
[[./Vassal|Vassal]] - You have made a binding pact with a powerful patron, an entity, or a higher power.
