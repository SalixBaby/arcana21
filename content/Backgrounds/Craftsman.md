>_Characters with the **Craftsman** background are skilled in the production of tools, weapons, or other goods. They are masters of their trade and know how to create and repair complex objects with their hands._

**Possible Traits:** Repair, production, knowledge of raw materials

**Blessing: Master of the Craft**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]] or [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]]
- Knowledge in Mathematics/Engineering
- Proficiency with [[../Equipment/Toolkits/Leathercraft Toolkit|Leathercraft Toolkit]], [[../Equipment/Toolkits/Stonemason's Toolkit|Stonemason's Toolkit]], or [[../Equipment/Toolkits/Smithing Toolkit|Smithing Toolkit]]
- Half the time required for repairs

**Curse: Tunnel Vision**
- Gets lost in work and projects
- Often pays little attention to social interactions
    - Optional: Perfectionism
