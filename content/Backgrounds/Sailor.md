>_Characters with the **Sailor** background are accustomed to life at sea. They know the dangers of the ocean and are well practiced at weathering storms and navigating their crew through rough waters._

**Possible Traits:** Navigation, seamanship, maritime knowledge

**Blessing: Wind and Waves**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]] or [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]]
- Knowledge in Survival/Orientation
- Knowledge of nautical science and meteorology

**Curse: Landlocked**
- Discomfort during extended stays away from water
- Difficulty finding one's way in cities
    - Optional: Wanderlust
