>_Merchants are skilled traders and excellent negotiating partners. They know the tricks and pitfalls of commerce and understand how to handle goods and coin to maximize their profit._

**Possible Traits:** Negotiation, arithmetic, knowledge of goods

**Blessing: Business Sense**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge and bonus on Trade/Economics
- Access to merchant networks and discounts

**Curse: Greed**
- Tendency to use others for profit
- Distrust of strangers who might take advantage of them
    - Optional: Unscrupulousness
