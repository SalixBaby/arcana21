>_The **Military** background describes a character who has served in an army and is disciplined as well as battle-hardened. These individuals know the drill and hardships of soldier life and know how to apply their tactics and abilities in any battle._

**Possible Traits:** Weapon handling, tactics, command skills

**Blessing: Battle-Hardened**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]]
- Knowledge in Military/Tactics
- Proficiency with one additional weapon

**Curse: Combat Trauma**
- Nightmares and memories of past battles
- Difficulty with social interaction in times of peace
    - Optional: Overreaction to threats
