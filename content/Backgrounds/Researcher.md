>_The **Researcher** is constantly in search of new knowledge and adventure. These characters often traverse unknown territories or engage in scientific experiments to unveil the mysterious._

**Possible Traits:** Research, scientific methodology, analysis

**Blessing: Curiosity**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]]
- Knowledge in Mathematics/Engineering
- +1 Training Point
- Access to scientific equipment

**Curse: Obsession**
- Acquiring new knowledge takes precedence over everything else
- Difficulty setting priorities
    - Optional: Recklessness
