>*The **Vassal** background describes a character who has made a pact with a powerful entity – be it a demon, a god, an archmage, or another supernatural patron. In exchange for loyalty or services, the character has received special powers or knowledge, but is also bound to the will of this higher power. A vassal is both powerful and shackled to the goals of their patron, and the character must constantly find a balance between their own freedom and service to the entity.*

**Possible Traits:** Supernatural powers, knowledge from other worlds, obligations toward a higher power.

**Blessing: Power of the Pact**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]] or [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge in Religion/Myths or History/Languages
- Additional Magical Domain [[../Magical Domains/Soul/Curse|Curse]]
- The pact grants certain abilities thematically fitting to your character concept, which must be agreed upon with the GM.

**Curse: Fulfillment of the Pact**
- Obligation toward the higher entity
- Can lead to moral conflicts or dangerous assignments
	- Optional: Loss of power if the entity is displeased
