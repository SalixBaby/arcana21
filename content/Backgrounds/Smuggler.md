>_The **Smuggler** knows the secret routes and paths for transporting illegal goods and trading beyond the reach of authorities. These characters are clever, inconspicuous, and possess a solid network in the underworld._

**Possible Traits:** Concealment, stealth, illegal trade

**Blessing: Underground Contacts**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge and Bonus in Trade/Economics
- Access to black markets and illegal goods

**Curse: Outlaw**
- Constantly on the run from authorities
- Difficulty earning the trust of law-abiding individuals
    - Optional: Greed for wealth
