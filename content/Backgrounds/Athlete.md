> **Athlete** describes someone devoted to physical training and competition. Characters with this background have shaped their endurance, strength, and dexterity through years of training, whether in single combat, races, martial arts, or another athletic discipline. They know the limits of their body and how to push past them.

**Possible Traits:** Physical resilience, quick recovery, excellent body control, strong competitive spirit.

**Blessing: Champion's Resolve**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]] or [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]]
- +1 on checks requiring physical endurance or performance
- Basic knowledge of various competitions and athletic tactics

**Curse: Tunnel Vision**
- Excessive ambition in competitions, often reluctant to be the first to give up
- Perfectionist when it comes to competing
	- Optional: "Headfirst Through the Wall"
