>*The **Cultist** background describes characters who grew up in a sect, a secret society, or a hidden circle of faith, or were part of one in their early years. Whether through birth, indoctrination, or conscious choice – the cult has left its mark: secret knowledge, rituals, and a distorted view of power, truth, or morality. Even after breaking with the cult, memories, influences, and old connections remain.*

**Possible Traits:** Occult teachings, secret rituals, conspiracy, mental discipline

**Blessing: Occult Knowledge**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge in Religion/Myths and Cryptology/Codes
- Access to hidden cults, sect knowledge, and secret networks

**Curse: Marked**
- Distrust from outsiders
- Mental aftereffects of indoctrination
    - Optional: Persecution by the former cult
