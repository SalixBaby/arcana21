>_The **Gambler** is a cunning risk-taker who knows the tricks and techniques to swing luck in their favor and loves the thrill of bets and games._

**Possible Traits:** Luck, manipulation, sharp wit

**Blessing: Lucky Streak**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge in Trade/Economics
- Bonus on Luck checks
- Access to gambling networks

**Curse: Gambling Addiction**
- Difficulty resisting a risk
- Heavy debts from lost bets
    - Optional: Impulsivity
