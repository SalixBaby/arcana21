>_The background of **Nature** describes someone who lives in close connection with the wilderness. They know the secrets of the forest, the mountains, or the plains and understand how to use natural resources to survive. Their deep knowledge of flora and fauna helps them navigate the wild._

**Possible Traits:** Wilderness survival, knowledge of animals and plants, tracking, nature-attuned magic.

**Blessing: One with the Wild**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge and Bonus in Nature/Creatures
- Knowledge of herbalism and medicinal plants

**Curse: Hermit**
- Discomfort in urban environments
- Distrust of technology or civilization
	- Optional: Aversion to strangers
