>_Characters with the **Street Urchin** background grew up on the streets and alleyways of the city. They are accustomed to a rough life and know the tricks for surviving in the underworld. These characters are often nimble, clever, and constantly alert._

**Possible Traits:** Pickpocketing, street knowledge, stealth

**Blessing: Light-Fingered**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Body/-Body-|Body]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge and Bonus in Survival/Orientation
- Knows local underworld contacts

**Curse: Distrust**
- Difficulty trusting others
- Difficulty moving in upper-class circles
    - Optional: Bitterness
