>_The **Faith** background describes characters who have devoted themselves to a religion or spiritual path. They may be priests, temple servants, or devout wanderers who spread the teachings of their faith and strengthen the morale of the group._

**Possible Traits:** Religious knowledge, ritualism, spiritual healing

**Blessing: Divine Blessing**
- +1 to [[../Attributes/Mind/-Mind-|Mind]] or [[../Attributes/Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]
- Knowledge and bonus on Religion/Myths
- Access to religious beings and repositories of knowledge

**Curse: Dogmatic**
- Strict moral rules restrict behavior
- Conflicts with those of different faiths
    - Optional: Narrow-mindedness
