> _The **Body** represents the physical abilities of a character. It encompasses everything related to physical strength, endurance, and movement. Physical attributes determine how well a character interacts with their environment, whether in combat, athletic challenges, or overcoming obstacles. This category is crucial for characters who rely on raw power, speed, and physical superiority._
