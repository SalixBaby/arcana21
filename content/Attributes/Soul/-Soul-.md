> _The **Soul** represents the emotional and social abilities of a character. It describes how a character interacts with others, influences people, and understands the depths of the human or supernatural psyche. This category is about empathy, persuasiveness, and intuitive understanding. Characters who excel in social or spiritual areas draw their strength from the Soul._
