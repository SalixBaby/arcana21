> _The attributes of your character determine their abilities and are the core of their profile. They are divided into **core attributes**, **properties** (sub-attributes), and **knowledge**. Each attribute represents specific strengths, weaknesses, and skills that shape your character in various situations._

---

#### **1. Core Attributes**

The three core attributes – **Body**, **Mind**, and **Soul** – form the foundation of all your character's abilities. They reflect fundamental physical, mental, and emotional aspects.

1. **[[./Body/-Body-|Body]]** The _Body_ attribute represents the physical abilities of your character. It stands for strength, endurance, and the ability to move quickly and precisely.
    - Influences Health, Movement, and endurance.

2. **[[./Mind/-Mind-|Mind]]** The _Mind_ attribute stands for intellect, knowledge, and magical understanding. It represents the ability to think analytically, understand the environment, and cast spells.
    - Influences Mana points, Perception and mental resilience.

3. **[[./Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]** The _Soul_ attribute stands for charisma, empathy, and intuition. It represents the ability to interact with others, read emotions, and persuade people.
    - Influences the strength of social interactions and inspiration of others.

---

#### **2. Properties (Sub-Attributes)**

The core attributes are each divided into five properties that describe more specific abilities of your character.

**[[./Body/-Body-|Body]]:**

- **[[./Body/Constitution|Constitution]]:** Physical endurance and resistance. Influences Health and resistance against physical influences.
- **[[./Body/Dexterity|Dexterity]]:** Precise movements and hand-eye coordination. Influences physical attacks in combat.
- **[[./Body/Instinct|Instinct]]:** Ability to react quickly and sense danger. Influences creative checks and initiative.
- **[[./Body/Speed|Speed]]:** Movement pace and reaction ability. Influences Dodge and Movement.
- **[[./Body/Strength|Strength]]:** Physical power. Influences jump distance, grappling, and immobilizing opponents.

**[[./Mind/-Mind-|Mind]]:**

- **[[./Mind/Arcana|Arcana]]:** Knowledge and control over magical forces. Influences casting and overloading spells.
- **[[./Mind/Experience|Experience]]:** Practical knowledge and problem-solving ability. Influences creative checks and initiative.
- **[[./Mind/Focus|Focus]]:** Concentration ability and mental strength. Influences spell stability and willpower.
- **[[./Mind/Knowledge|Knowledge]]:** General education and intellectual depth. Grants access to specific fields of expertise.
- **[[./Mind/Perception|Perception]]:** Ability to notice details in the environment. Influences detection and surprise.

**[[./Soul/-Soul-|Soul]]:**

- **[[./Soul/Deception|Deception]]:** Skill in deceiving and manipulating. Influences bluffing and intrigue.
- **[[./Soul/Insight|Insight]]:** Ability to read the emotions and intentions of others. Influences social interactions.
- **[[./Soul/Intuition|Intuition]]:** Gut feeling and the ability to make spontaneous decisions. Influences creative checks and initiative.
- **[[./Soul/Performance|Performance]]:** Charisma and presence. Influences how others perceive you.
- **[[./Soul/Persuasion|Persuasion]]:** Ability to convince others of an idea or course of action. Influences negotiations and diplomacy.

#### **3. Knowledge (Sub-category of Mind)**

The _Knowledge_ attribute is divided into 12 specialized categories representing specific expertise.

##### **Art / Culture**

Knowledge of artistic styles, traditions, social norms, and cultural peculiarities. Ideal for appraising works of art, understanding rituals, or avoiding misunderstandings in foreign societies.

---

##### **Craftsmanship / Metallurgy**

Knowledge of craft processes, smithing techniques, and the properties of various materials and ores. Indispensable for crafting weapons or tools, determining metal quality, or repairing technical constructions.

---

##### **Cryptology / Codes**

Knowledge of codes, encrypted messages, symbolism, and the art of deciphering secret writing. Useful for decoding hidden messages, recognizing magical or mundane sigils, or exposing covert communication.

---

##### **History / Languages**

Knowledge of historical events, cultures, and ancient languages. Allows deciphering old texts, recognizing historical contexts, and classifying cultural relics.

---

##### **Mathematics / Engineering**

Skills in logic, mechanics, physics, and technical constructions. Essential for developing devices, solving puzzles with precise calculations, or understanding complex mechanisms.

---

##### **Medicine / Anatomy**

Knowledge of the physical structure of various creatures, healing methods, and the treatment of injuries. Indispensable for treating wounds, producing medical remedies, or identifying poisons and diseases.

---

##### **Military / Tactics**

Strategic understanding of warfare, battle structure, and the command of units. Important for developing effective strategies, analyzing opponents, or positioning troops effectively.

---

##### **Nature / Creatures**

Understanding of flora, fauna, monster lore, and ecological relationships. Useful for taming animals, reading tracks, identifying dangerous creatures, or correctly using plants.

---

##### **Politics / Diplomacy**

Knowledge of power structures, negotiation tactics, and social dynamics between groups or nations. Perfect for recognizing intrigue, conducting diplomatic talks, or forging political alliances.

---

##### **Religion / Myths**

Knowledge of belief systems, deities, rituals, sacred symbols, and supernatural traditions. Allows interpreting religious signs, understanding mythical contexts, or recognizing occult practices.

---

##### **Survival / Orientation**

Skills for living in the wilderness: navigation, camp building, tracking, and hazard assessment. Essential for finding your way in unknown territory, planning safe routes, or avoiding deadly situations.

---

##### **Trade / Economics**

Understanding of resources, markets, trade flows, and economic dynamics. Helpful for negotiating fair prices, planning trade routes, or recognizing economic manipulation.

---

#### **4. Additional Attributes and Values**

Alongside the core attributes and their properties, there are further important values that are crucial in the game:

- **Health:** The life energy of your character, influenced by Constitution.
- **Mana Points:** The resource for magic users, influenced by Arcana.
- **Movement:** How far your character can move per round, influenced by Speed.
- **Dodge:** The ability to evade attacks, influenced by Speed.
- **Willpower:** Mental resistance against influence, influenced by Focus.
- **Endurance Points:** Resources for additional actions or special maneuvers, based on the Body value.
- **Inspiration Points:** Resource to reroll a check, based on the Soul value.
