Describes the degree of academic, historical, or specialized expertise a character possesses. This attribute is particularly valuable in situations where information and problem-solving drawn from books or study are important.

_Divided into the following categories:_

- **Art / Culture**
- **Craftsmanship / Metallurgy**
- **Cryptology / Codes**
- **History / Languages**
- **Mathematics / Engineering**
- **Medicine / Anatomy**
- **Military / Tactics**
- **Nature / Creatures**
- **Politics / Diplomacy**
- **Religion / Myths**
- **Survival / Orientation**
- **Trade / Economics**
